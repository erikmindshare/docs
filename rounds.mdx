# Rounds

A round is one sending of the same survey. Reports put the rounds side by side, so you see if a score went up or down. A question can also show in only some rounds, for example a question about a new topic from Q2. The wording of a question is the same in every round.

<Note>
Interview Mode is in development. It is not available in installed AutoSurvey orgs yet. This page tells you what the interview does when it is available.
</Note>

## When the interview asks about rounds

The interview asks this question only when your brief says that you send the survey again and compare the results. Examples are "every quarter and compare", "before and after" and "track over time". If the brief does not say this, the interview does not ask.

The question is: **Will you send this survey again and compare the results?**

The interview does not pre-select an answer for this topic. You must click a choice.

## The choices

| Choice | What you see | What AutoSurvey changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Yes, the same questions each round** (recommended) | AutoSurvey makes the rounds. Every round asks the same questions, so you can compare them. | Turns on the survey setting **Has Rounds**. |
| **Yes, and some questions change by round** | AutoSurvey makes the rounds. After Create, a Set up next card opens the questions. In the ⋯ menu, Manage audiences & waves sets the questions for each round. | Turns on **Has Rounds**. After Create, the **Choose the questions for each round** card shows. |
| **No, one survey** | AutoSurvey makes one survey with no rounds. | Sets **Has Rounds** to off. |

At **Finish**, each change shows as a change card with a **Keep** box. **Apply** makes only the kept changes. If the survey already has the value, no card shows. Before Create, the value waits on the draft, and Create writes it to the survey. After Create, **Apply** writes it to the survey.

## The Set up next card

The choice **Yes, and some questions change by round** adds the card **Choose the questions for each round** to the **Set up next** list after Create.

| Card part | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **What and why** | You chose rounds with different questions. On the Questions tab, open the ⋯ menu and click Manage audiences & waves. There you choose the questions for each round. |
| **Who** | You, or an admin |
| **Link** | **Open the questions**. It opens the survey workspace with this survey. |

The card has **Dismiss** and **Don't suggest this again**. **Don't suggest this again** applies to this survey only.
