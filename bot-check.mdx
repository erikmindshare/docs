---
title: "Bot check and Shield"
description: "Protect a public survey from bots and low-quality answers. Use start caps and CAPTCHA for every public link, and the Fraud & Quality Shield to score, flag, hold or block responses."
---

A public link lets all people answer, and it lets bots answer too. AutoSurvey has two layers of
protection:

1. **Start caps and CAPTCHA.** These limit how many surveys can start in a period, and they can
   challenge a respondent. They apply to public links.
2. **Fraud & Quality Shield.** Shield scores each response for fraud and low quality. It flags,
   holds or blocks a risky response, and a person reviews it.

## Start caps and CAPTCHA

- The start cap is on with no setup. See [Security and privacy](/admin/security-and-privacy).
- A CAPTCHA challenge needs reCAPTCHA keys. See [CAPTCHA Keys](/admin/setup#captcha-keys).
- Each survey has the field **CAPTCHA Mode** with the values **Auto**, **Always** and **Never**.

## Fraud & Quality Shield

### Turn it on

An admin opens the **Setup** tab, opens **Features** and turns on the card **Fraud &
Quality Shield**. The feature is off until an admin turns it on. The switch gives the permission set
**AutoSurvey Shield Admin** to the AutoSurvey admins. Give **AutoSurvey Shield Reviewer** to each
person who reviews responses.

Use the tabs **Shield**, **Shield Review** and **Shield Settings**.

### Make a policy

A policy holds the rules and the limits. Open **Shield Settings**.

1. In **Policies**, enter a **New policy name** and click **Create policy**.
2. Set the limits:

   | Field | Meaning |
   | --- | --- |
   | **Monitor only** | On: Shield records each decision and blocks nothing. Off: Shield applies the decisions. |
   | **Flag at** | The score at which Shield flags a response. |
   | **Hold at** | The score at which Shield holds a response for review. It must be more than **Flag at**. |
   | **Block at** | The score at which Shield blocks a response. It must be more than **Hold at**. |
   | **Challenge provider** | reCAPTCHA v2, reCAPTCHA v3 or Cloudflare Turnstile. |
   | **Challenge when** | **Never**, **Always** or **When the risk goes up**. |
   | **Most runs per email per form** | The most responses from one email address for one form. |
   | **Most runs per email per day** | The most responses from one email address in one day. |
   | **Most runs per device per form** | The most responses from one device for one form. |
   | **AI review** | **On**: Einstein reads the open-text answers and adds a risk score and a quality score. It does not run until **Test Einstein** passes. |
   | **Einstein may raise the decision to** | **Do not raise**, **Flag** or **Hold**. It needs **AI review** on. |

3. In **Rules**, set **On**, **Weight** and **Hard** for each rule. The column **What it measures**
   explains each rule. The rules HONEYPOT_FILLED and CHALLENGE_FAILED are always on.
   HONEYPOT_FILLED finds a bot that fills in a field that people cannot see.
4. Click **Save policy**.

To see what a change does before you rely on it, enter the **Replay days** and click **Replay these
settings**. Shield shows the past decisions beside the decisions "With these settings".

### Protect a survey

A policy does nothing until it protects a form or a survey. Below the policy, find the section that
starts with **Protected forms**. Click the **Protect** button, find the form or the survey, select
**On** and click **Save binding**.

### Add the challenge keys

In **Challenge provider keys**, enter the **Secret key** and click **Save key**, then **Test key**.
Enter the **Site key** and click **Save site key**. A provider needs the two keys.

### Let Einstein review the answers

In **Einstein AI review**, enter the API name of a Prompt Builder template that has one input named
Answers. Click **Test Einstein**. Tell your respondents if AI reviews their answers.

### Review responses

Reviewers open the tab **Shield Review** and click **Approve**, **Reject** or **Duplicate** for each
response. The tab **Shield** shows the totals for a period.
