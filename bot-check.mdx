# Bot check

A honeypot is a trap for bots. It is not a reward. The survey has one extra field that people cannot see, so a person leaves it empty. A bot fills in every field that it finds. AutoSurvey rejects a response that has the hidden field filled in. People see nothing and do nothing. A captcha is a second check that people can see. People tick "I am human" before Submit (reCAPTCHA or Turnstile).

<Note>
Interview Mode is in development. It is not available in installed AutoSurvey orgs yet. This page tells you what the interview does when it is available.
</Note>

## Before you start: Fraud & Quality Shield

The honeypot check runs in the **Fraud & Quality Shield** feature. Shield is off by default. While Shield is off in your org, the interview shows the Bot check topic as turned off:

- If you can turn features on, click **Turn on Fraud & Quality Shield**. AutoSurvey Setup opens in a new tab at the feature card. Turn on its switch there, then come back. The screen checks again when you come back.
- If you cannot, the screen says to ask your admin. **Copy a message to your admin** copies a message that you can send.
- **Continue without it for now** goes on with the interview.

## When the interview asks

The interview asks this question for a survey with a public link, a survey that "anyone can answer", or a survey with a reward. The question is: **Protect the survey from bots?**

## The choices

| Choice | What you see | What AutoSurvey changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Honeypot only** (recommended) | A hidden field catches bots. People see nothing. | **CAPTCHA Mode** = **Never**. The survey gets an enabled Shield policy binding. |
| **Honeypot and captcha** | A visible captcha is added. Use it with a reward. | **CAPTCHA Mode** = **Always**. The survey gets an enabled Shield policy binding. |
| **No bot check** | AutoSurvey adds no extra check. | **CAPTCHA Mode** = **Never**. No binding is made. |

At **Finish**, each change shows as a change card with a **Keep** box. **Apply** makes only the kept changes.

<Note>
**Honeypot only** and **No bot check** set **CAPTCHA Mode** to **Never**. Then the survey does not show a captcha, also not on a burst of starts. The default mode **Auto** can show one. See [Security and Privacy](/admin/security-and-privacy).
</Note>

## The Shield policy binding

Shield checks a survey only through an enabled policy binding. A CAPTCHA Mode value alone does not check the honeypot. So a honeypot choice does this:

- If the survey already has a Shield binding, AutoSurvey keeps it. It does not replace a binding that an admin made.
- If not, AutoSurvey binds the survey to a policy that it makes the first time. That policy blocks a filled honeypot and a failed challenge, and nothing else.
- If you do not have Shield admin access, AutoSurvey cannot make the binding. The change list then says: "The honeypot is not on yet. Open Shield and bind a policy to this survey."

## If Shield is off

If a Bot check answer reaches **Finish** while Shield is off, AutoSurvey does not change the survey. The change goes to the **Set up next** list. The card says: "You chose this in the interview. The feature is turned off in this org. An admin can turn it on in Setup." Its link is **Open AutoSurvey Setup**.
