This page is for the person who receives a link, for example Jordan Rivera at Harbor Community Health. Staff can share this page with a client.

## Open the link

1. Open the link in the message from the office. It works on a phone or a computer.
2. The page shows the name of the office (for example Harbor Community Health), "Documents for" and your name, and a list of items.

Each item shows one of these states: **To do**, **Waiting for review**, **Accepted**, **Needs a new one**, **Not needed**. A bar shows your progress.

If the link does not work, the page says "This link cannot be opened". Then it says: "If you still need to send documents, ask the office that sent you the link for a new one." A link can fail because it is wrong, expired, replaced by a newer link, or cancelled.

## Send a photo or a file

1. Select **Start** on the item.
2. Select **Take a photo or choose a file**. You can use the camera, or choose an image or a PDF.
3. The page checks the picture on your device. This check is only advice. The page shows **Looks readable.** or one of these messages:
   - "This looks like a blank page"
   - "Too dark to read"
   - "Too bright or glare on the page"
   - "That one looks blurry"
   - "A corner is cut off"

   Each message has three tips.
4. Choose **Retake**, **Choose a different one**, or **Send anyway and let staff decide**. If the picture is good, select **Send**.
5. The item changes to "Waiting for review".

The file must be a JPG, PNG or PDF. The size limit is 3.5 MB or less. The office can set a lower limit for an item, and the page names it (for example "Keep it under 3 MB."). If you tap Send two times, only one copy goes.

If the page says "That file type is not accepted. Use JPG, PNG or PDF.", "That file is too large. Keep it under 3.5 MB." or "That file is empty.", choose a different file.

## Acknowledgment

1. Type your name as it should appear.
2. Tick the box.
3. Draw a signature if the page offers it. This step is optional.
4. Select **Send**.

The system accepts this item immediately. If you do not tick the box, the page says "Tick the box to confirm before you send." If you do not type the name, it says "Type your name as it should appear."

## Forms

A form item opens the questions on the same page. Your answers save each time you go to the next page.

<Warning>
Do not leave a one-page form half done. A one-page form can lose answers if you reload the page.
</Warning>

After you send:

- If staff must review, the item says "Waiting for review".
- If not, it says "Accepted".
- The page says "Thanks. Your answers are in." Some forms say "Thanks. Your answers are in. Staff will contact you."

If a form is not ready, the page says "This item is in development. It cannot be opened yet."

If you open the same item in a second tab, the page says "This item is waiting for review." or "This item is closed. It cannot be changed."

## Sign a document

1. **Read the document.**
2. Type your full name (up to 120 characters).
3. Draw your signature, or type it. Choose **Draw** or **Type**.
4. Read the disclosure text.
5. Tick "I agree to sign this document electronically."
6. Select **Sign and send**.

### Sign for someone else

Some documents let you sign for another person. If so, you see "I am signing for someone else".

1. Tick the box.
2. Choose "Parent or guardian" or "Authorized representative".
3. Type your **own** full name (the person who signs).
4. Confirm the statement "I confirm that I have the authority to sign this document for" followed by the name of the person you sign for.

The page says: "The office does not check your identity or your authority when you sign here." The certificate shows "Identity and authority: Not verified".

### After you sign

The page says "Thanks. Your signed document is in." The system then makes the PDF. This can take a short time. The page has a **Check again** button while it waits. The page shows the Document ID, what you signed, what you entered, and a certificate.

If a document has a character that the PDF cannot print, the page tells you that you cannot sign it online. Nothing is wrong with your entry. Ask the office for a paper copy to sign. Staff can upload the signed paper copy for you.

## If staff send an item back

The item says "Staff asked for a new one:" and the reason. For a signed document, it says "Please sign again:" and the reason. Select **Replace** and send a new one.

## When you are done

When every item is accepted, the page says "All done. Every item is accepted. Thank you." If staff waived an item, it says "All done. Thank you." The page is then read-only.

The page footer says: "In development. Nothing you send here leaves the organization's Salesforce org."

## Using a staff device

If you use a staff member's tablet, the page says: "You are using a staff member's device. Your answers go only to this request." It also shows when the session ends (30 minutes after it starts). When you finish, tap **Finish and hand back**. The page says "You are done on this device. Please give the device back to the staff member."
