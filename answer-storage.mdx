# Where answers are stored

AutoSurvey stores answers in a Salesforce object. The default name comes from the survey title. You can choose a different name before you create the survey. After Create, the name cannot change.

<Note>
Interview Mode is in development. It is not available in installed AutoSurvey orgs yet. This page tells you what the interview does when it is available.
</Note>

## When the interview asks

The topic is in the group **Survey basics**. The interview asks it only when the plan for your brief names it. It does not show in the list of all topics.

The screen selects the recommended choice first. You can click a different choice.

## The choices

| Choice | What you see | What AutoSurvey changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Use the name from the title** (recommended) | AutoSurvey names the object from the survey title. | Nothing. Create names the object. |
| **Choose my own name** | AutoSurvey uses the name you give. It ends in __c. | No change from the click. The screen has no box for the name. The AI can propose a name at **Finish** from your brief or from your text in **Anything else to change?** |

When the AI proposes a name, it shows as a change card. AutoSurvey adds __c to the end of the name. The name has 40 characters at most.

## Change it later

After Create, the name cannot change. The interview refuses a name change after Create. To get a different name, ask for it before Create, in your brief or in **Anything else to change?** The **Reporting object** field is in the **Data** section of the survey **Settings** tab. See [Survey settings](/admin/survey-settings).

## Limits

- The screen has no box to type the name. The name comes only from the AI.
