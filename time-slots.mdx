---
title: "Program Slot Booking"
description: "Publish appointment times for a program and send each person a booking link. The person books, changes or cancels a time on a phone, with no account."
---

A time-slot question lets people select a time, for example for a visit or a call. At Finish, the AI adds a choice question with times. It uses the times that your brief names. If your brief names no times, check and change the times in the Questions tab. The question does not keep a seat, and it has no limit for each time.

For times that have a limit of people, use **Program Slot Booking**.

Staff lose time when they arrange intake appointments by phone and email. With **Program Slot
Booking**, staff publish the open times one time. Each person gets a link, picks a time on a phone
and can change or cancel it. A full time shows as **Full**.

Program Slot Booking is a tool of its own, on the tab **Slot Booking**. It is not a question type
in a survey.

## Before you start

- An admin turns on the feature. Open the **Setup** tab, open **Optional Capabilities**, and turn on
  the card **Program Slot Booking**. The feature is off until an admin turns it on.
- The switch gives **AutoSurvey Slot Booking Admin** to the AutoSurvey admins. This set shows only the
  tab **Slot Booking**.
- An admin assigns **Slot Booking Staff** to the staff, and **Slot Booking Guest Access** to the guest
  user of the Experience site. Without the guest set, a booking link does not open.
- **Setup health** at the top of the tab shows each check.

## Add times

1. Open the tab **Slot Booking**.
2. Select the program in **Inventory**.
3. On the tab **Times**, find **Generate slots** and fill in these fields:

   | Field | Meaning |
   | --- | --- |
   | **First day** | The date of the first slots. |
   | **Days** | The number of days to make slots for. |
   | **Start hour** | The hour of the first slot of each day. |
   | **Per day** | The number of slots on each day. |
   | **Minutes** | The length of one slot. |
   | **Capacity** | The number of people who can book one slot. |
   | **Staff** | The name of the staff member for the slots. |

4. Click **Add draft slots**.
5. Click **Publish inventory**.

## Change the times

| To do this | Click |
| --- | --- |
| Publish one slot | **Publish** |
| Close one slot | **Close** |
| Change how many people can book a slot | **Capacity**, enter the **New capacity**, then **Save** |
| Cancel a slot that has bookings | **Cancel slot**, enter the **Reason for the cancellation**, then **Cancel this time** |
| Change all slots of one series | **Publish series**, **Unpublish series** or **Close series** |
| Stop or start all booking for the program | **Close program** or **Open program** |

## Send a booking link

1. In **Booking link**, select the **Contact**.
2. Enter the **Name shown on the page**.
3. Click **Create booking link**. Send the link to the person.

## What the person sees

The page shows **Nearest open times**. The person picks a time. The page then says "Your time is
held. Finish the questions below to complete your intake." When the intake is complete, the page
shows **Intake complete**.

The person can use **Change time** and **Cancel booking**. When no time is open, the person can
click **Ask to be told when a time opens**.

## The roster

The tab **Roster** shows the people who booked the sessions of one **Day**. Mark each person
**Attended**, **No-show** or **Excused**. You can also use **Move**, **Cancel booking** and **Withdraw**.
**Download roster (CSV)** saves the list of one session.

## The interest list

The tab **Interest list** shows the people who asked to be told when a time opens.

- **Book a time** books an open time for a person on the list.
- **Remove from the list** takes a person off the list.
- **Close the interest list** closes the list. AutoSurvey asks you to confirm.

## Notices that did not send

The tab **Notices** lists each notice that did not send after every attempt. Click **Try again** for
one notice or **Try all again** for all.

## Limits

- The **Inventory** list shows the first 1000 programs. Close or archive old programs to see the rest.
- The board shows the first 500 times of a program.
