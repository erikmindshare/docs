# Time slots

A time-slot question shows the open times. A full slot is hidden.

<Note>
Interview Mode is in development. It is not available in installed AutoSurvey orgs yet. This page tells you what the interview does when it is available.
</Note>

## Before you start

This topic belongs to the feature **Program Slot Booking**. It is off by default. An admin turns it on in AutoSurvey Setup. When the feature is off, the interview shows the topic as turned off.

## When the interview asks

The interview asks this question when your brief says that people pick a time, or names sessions or limited seats.

The question is: **Let people pick a time slot?** The screen selects the recommended choice first. You can click a different choice.

## The choices

| Choice | What you see | What AutoSurvey changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Yes, add a time-slot question** (recommended) | AutoSurvey adds a slot question. You set the slots after Create. | No setting changes. The answer goes to the AI at **Finish**. |
| **No** | The survey has no slot question. | Nothing. |

This topic uses the AI at **Finish**. The AI gets your answer and its result text. It can reword, remove or add questions, change the answer options and change a question type. Each proposal is a change card that you keep or skip. If the AI proposes nothing, no change card shows.

## Limits

- The AI cannot add a time-slot question. It has no time-slot question type. It can add a plain question, for example a choice list of times, but that question has no seat limit.
- This topic adds no Set up next card. Add the time-slot question yourself in the survey builder after Create.
