# AI themes on open answers

The AI groups the open answers into themes, for example "Too long" or "Great presenter". Each theme shows a count.

<Note>
The interview does not ask this question. To read the open answers of a survey, see [Results and responses](/concepts/results-and-responses).
</Note>

## Status

- AutoSurvey does not register a **ai-themes** feature in this release. The interview treats a topic with an unknown feature as not installed, and it never asks a topic that is not installed. The topic also does not show in the list of all topics.
- If a choice for this topic reaches the change list, it does not change the survey. It goes to the **Set up next** list as a card that says: "You chose this in the interview. This feature is not in AutoSurvey yet. The choice is kept for when it ships."
- AI themes are not in AutoSurvey yet.

## The question and the choices

When the feature is released, the interview asks: **Group the open answers into themes?** The interview is to ask it when your survey has many open-text questions.

| Choice | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| **Yes, group the answers** | The AI groups open answers into themes. |
| **No** (recommended) | Open answers stay as they are. |
