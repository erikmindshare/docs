---
title: "Google Sheets sync"
description: "What the Google Sheet of a survey holds, and where to get the answers."
---

Google Sheets sync copies each response to a Google Sheet. Teams that work in Sheets see new answers
at once.

The Google Sheet of a survey holds the questions. AutoSurvey does not copy the answers to a Google
Sheet.

- To write or change the questions in a sheet, see [Sheets to Survey](/workflows/sheets-to-flow).
- To read or export the answers, see [Results and responses](/concepts/results-and-responses).
- To use the answers in another system, see [Integrating with AutoSurvey](/api/overview).
