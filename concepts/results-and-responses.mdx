title: "Results and Responses"
description: "What the Results tab shows, how to browse and export every response, and how to read one response in the Response Viewer."
---

AutoSurvey shows your responses in three places. The **Results** tab of a survey gives a summary.
The **Responses** page lists every response, with filters, Insights and Export. The **Response
Viewer** shows one response, answer by answer.

## The Results tab

In the **Surveys** tab, open a survey and click **Results**. It is the tab after **Send**.

### Responses

The **Responses** section starts with the count of finished responses, for example "214 responses",
across all releases. Click **Refresh** to read the numbers again. A second click on the **Results**
tab does the same.

Below the count:

* **Sends** — how many sends produced these responses, and the latest ones. **See sends** opens the
  **Send** tab. With no sends, the line says "No sends yet" and the link is **Go to Send**.
* **Accepting since** or **Closed since** — when collection started or stopped. **Stop accepting responses…** and
  **Reopen collection…** are here too, for a person who can manage the survey.

### Answers so far

**Answers so far** lists every question in survey order, with how many responses answered it and a
short summary:

| Question kind | Summary |
| --- | --- |
| NPS | The score and its parts, for example "NPS +40 · 12 promoters · 5 passives · 3 detractors" |
| Choice | The top four options by count, then "and N more". A scored option set also shows its score |
| Checkbox | **Yes** and **No** by count |
| Number, Rating, Percent, Currency | The average and the range. A Percent answer shows "%" and a Currency answer shows its currency |
| Date and time | The earliest and the latest answer, in your Salesforce time zone |
| Text | The two newest answers. **Read all N** opens all answers in place; **Show less** closes them |

A masked answer shows "••••••", never its value. A rich-text answer shows its formatting. You need
raw response access to read text answers here. **View all responses ›** opens the full list.

### Digest

Below the answers, the digest shows:

* **finished**, **started**, and the percentage "of those who started finished".
* **Primary score**, with "n = N" beside it. This shows only when the survey has a primary score
  question. **Combined score** shows when a combined score exists.
* "last response 2h ago", and a bar chart of recent days. **Show 30 days** widens it.
* **Channel results** — how many responses each channel started.

Use **Releases** to see one release only. The default is **All releases**. The live release says
"(live)". With one release chosen, a line says "Showing v2 · 214 responses".

### Links to more detail

* **View full results →** opens the **Responses** page for this survey.
* **Analyze:** **Insights** opens the same page with Insights open. **Export** opens it with finished,
  real responses chosen, so the file matches the number you saw. **CX Analytics** opens the CX
  Analytics page, filtered to this survey.
* **Reports ▾** opens the **Submissions** and **Answers** reports for this survey. It shows only for a
  person with raw response access, and only when the reports are installed.

### When there are no responses

Until the first response, the tab says "No responses yet. A response appears here when someone
finishes the survey. Use Send to share it."

<Note>
  Test runs are not counted. The count says "N test runs shown below, not counted", and the
  **Test runs** line at the bottom has a **See test runs** link.
</Note>

If some responses are not in the reporting object, the tab also shows a **Missing from Salesforce
reports** card with a **Retry** button per response. See
[Data & Reporting](/concepts/data-and-reporting#the-reporting-object-is-a-copy).

## The Responses page

**View full results →** opens the **Responses** page, titled "Responses · *survey name*". The
**Responses** tab in the app opens the same page for all surveys.

* **Filters** — **Status** (**All**, **Draft**, **Submitted**, **Invalid**), **Data Quality**
  (**All**, **Live**, **Excluded**, **Test**, **Ignored**, **AI Flagged**, with counts),
  **Activity** (**All**, **Today**, **7d**, **30d**, **90d**) and **Search** by name or code.
  **More filters** adds **Form**, **Audience**, **Wave**, **Release**, **Responder Type**,
  **Latest responses only** and **Show empty drafts**.
* **Chips** — each active filter shows as a chip, for example "Release: v2". Click its **×** to
  remove it. **Reset** clears all filters.
* **Saved view** — the page remembers your filters for the next time you open it. A link from the
  **Results** tab starts from its own filters.
* **Pages** — the footer says "1–25 of 500". Use **First**, **Back** and **Next**. **Rows per page**
  is **25**, **50** or **100**.
* **Dates** — **Last Saved** and **Submitted** show in your Salesforce time zone.

**Insights** shows totals, completion, submissions over time, drop-off by screen, choice
distribution, Net Promoter Score, numeric questions (count, average, min, max) and free-text
responses for one question at a time. It shows when a survey is chosen.

**Export** offers **Submission List (CSV)**, **Responses — Long Format (CSV)**, **Responses — Wide
Format (CSV)**, **Question Codebook (CSV)**, **Picklist Options (CSV)** and **Analysis Package
(ZIP)**. The dialog states what the file contains, for example "Exports finished responses for
*survey*, real responses only, all releases." You can choose an **Anonymous export** and **Include
Test/Ignored responses**. Click **Download**.

### Row actions

The menu at the end of each row has:

* **Open Response Viewer** — opens the response.
* **Open Flat Response** — opens the copy in the reporting object, when one exists.
* **Mark as Test**, **Mark as Ignored**, **Restore (clear data quality)** — needs the **Mark Data
  Quality** permission.
* **Delete** — needs the **Delete Submission** permission. It also deletes the reporting copy, and
  it cannot be undone.

## The Response Viewer

The Response Viewer shows one response, screen by screen.

* The title is the survey name. Click it to open that survey on its **Results** tab. **All
  responses** opens the **Responses** page for the survey.
* **Data Quality**, **Delete** and **Print** are at the top right, with the same permissions as on
  the **Responses** page.
* A masked answer is hidden. A person with the **Reveal Masked Answers** permission sees **Reveal
  masked values**. Masking hides the value on screen; it does not encrypt it.
* A rich-text answer shows its formatting.

The **⋯** menu has **Refresh**, **Copy all**, **Show Field Label**, **Compact density**, **Show
completion counts**, **Show unanswered** and **Show help text**.

<Tip>
  The **⋯** menu also has developer items (**Show Field API Name**, **Show Field Data Type**, **Show
  raw values**, **Debug**). They show only when an admin sets the component's mode to admin on the
  record page. The record page that AutoSurvey installs does not show them.
</Tip>
