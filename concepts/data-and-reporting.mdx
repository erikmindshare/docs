Responses are Salesforce records the moment they're submitted. There's no sync, no export, and
nothing to unpack before you can report.

## Three grains, and every response writes all of them

You don't choose a storage model up front. Every submission lands in all three, so you pick the one
that suits the question you're asking:

| Grain | One row per | Reach for it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Your Response Object** | Response, with a field per question | Ordinary reports, dashboards, roll-ups, list views |
| **Survey Submission** | Response — who, when, which release, what state | Completion rates, operational reporting, funnels |
| **Survey Answer** | Answer | Comparing across surveys, tracking a question over time, drop-off |

<Tip>
  **Start with the Response Object.** One field per question is what Salesforce reporting is good
  at, and most questions are answered there. Move to Survey Answer only when your question spans
  surveys or waves — that's where the wide layout stops helping.
</Tip>

## What ships ready to run

### Report types

Five custom report types, so you can build your own without joining anything by hand:

| Report type | Grain |
| --- | --- |
| **Survey Submissions with Answers** | Submissions with their answers, including the drop-off screen ordinal |
| **ASA Cross-Survey Answers** | Every answer across every survey — for pivots, trends and cross-survey comparison |
| **AutoSurvey CX Submissions (Scored)** | One row per submission with its stamped score, band, type, channel and program |
| **Survey Invitations with Campaign and Form** | The distribution funnel, by invitation |
| **Campaigns with Campaign Members and Survey Status** | Campaign funnel, with member survey rollups |

### Reports and a dashboard

| Folder | Contains |
| --- | --- |
| **AutoSurvey Survey Reports** | Completion by suite, submission status summary, answers in long format, campaign member status |
| **AutoSurvey CX Analytics** | Average NPS by channel, average NPS by month, NPS band distribution, and a detractor worklist — plus the **CX Analytics dashboard** built on them |
| **AutoSurvey Raw Response Reports** | Suite submissions and suite answers, at the individual-response level |

<Warning>
  The **Raw Response Reports** folder is deliberately restricted. It shows individual, unaggregated
  responses, so it needs the **Raw Response Access** permission — see
  [Installation & Permissions](/admin/installation-and-permissions). Aggregate reporting doesn't
  require it.
</Warning>

## Two things worth doing early

### Drop-off

**Survey Submissions with Answers** carries the **drop-off screen ordinal** — the page a respondent
stopped on. Grouping abandoned submissions by it tells you which page is costing you completions,
which is usually a more actionable number than the completion rate itself.

### Scores

The score and band stamped onto the submission — the ones the CX reports read — come from the one
question you designate as the form's **primary metric**: add `*` after its Metric Type in the
Questions Sheet (`NPS*`; `(Primary)` works too), at most one per form. A Metric Type on its own
(NPS, CSAT, CES or Rating) folds that answer into the submission's **Combined Score %** average, but
it doesn't stamp the primary score — and with no primary marked, the CX reports have nothing to show
for that submission. Marking your headline question is worth the thirty seconds. See
[The Questions Sheet](/concepts/questions-sheet).

## As surveys change

Reporting fields are only ever added, never removed — so a report built against last quarter's
survey keeps working after you edit it. The honest flip side: a question you delete keeps its field
and its collected answers. See
[What AutoSurvey Creates in Your Org](/admin/objects-permissions-layouts).

<Note>
  This is what makes the **Survey Answer** grain valuable over a long programme. Because it's one row
  per answer rather than one column per question, a question set that changes between waves doesn't
  change the shape of the data — so a three-wave trend is a report rather than a data project. See
  [Matrix programs](/concepts/matrix-programs).
</Note>
