The **Questions** tab is where a survey is written: one row per question, per section heading, per
page break. AutoSurvey reads **30** columns from it.

<Note>
  **Order does not matter and extra columns are ignored.** AutoSurvey finds each column by its
  header, so you can rearrange them, and your own working columns are left alone. Only three
  columns are required.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Header matching ignores case, spaces and punctuation — `Show If`, `show if`, `ShowIf` and
  `SHOW_IF` are the same column.
</Tip>

## The columns every question needs

| Column | Required | What it does | Also accepted as |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `API Name` | **Yes** | The field name this question stores its answer in. Must be unique across the suite, and it is what Show If rules and reports refer to. | `Field Name` |
| `Question` | **Yes** | The question text the respondent reads. | `Prompt` |
| `Display Type` | **Yes** | The control the respondent gets — text box, choice list, star rating. See [Display Types](/concepts/display-types). | — |

## Structure

| Column | Required | What it does | Also accepted as |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Screen` | No | Which page this row appears on. Rows sharing a value are on the same page; blank rows are all treated as one implicit page. | — |
| `Section` | No | Groups questions under a heading or card inside a page. | — |
| `Order` | No | The position of this row. Blank rows keep their sheet order. | — |

## Answers and choices

| Column | Required | What it does | Also accepted as |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Option Set` | No | The named choice list this question offers. See [Option Sets](/concepts/option-sets). On a Text Area question this column instead carries the height, as `lines:10`. | — |
| `Display Properties` | No | Per-question tuning — star count, slider range, upload limits. See [Display Properties](/concepts/display-properties). | — |
| `Allow Other` | No | Adds an "Other (please specify)" choice that reveals a free-text box. | — |
| `Other Label` | No | Custom wording for that Other choice. | — |
| `Default Value` | No | Pre-fills the answer. The respondent can change it. | — |
| `Data Type` | No | The coarse storage type. Normally derived from the Display Type — set it only when you need to override. | — |

## Behaviour

| Column | Required | What it does | Also accepted as |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Required` | No | `TRUE` means the respondent cannot move on without answering. | — |
| `Active` | No | `FALSE` keeps the row in your sheet but leaves it out of the survey. | — |
| `Show If` | No | Show or hide this row based on an earlier answer. See [Conditional Visibility](/concepts/conditional-visibility). | `Visibility`, `ShowIf`, `Dependency` |
| `Help Text` | No | A line of guidance shown under the question. | — |
| `Validation` | No | Constrains the answer — `3..200` for length, `range: 1..5` for a number, `regex: ^[A-Z]+$` for a pattern. | `Validation Regex`, `ValidationRegex`, `Validation Rules` |
| `Error Message` | No | The message shown when Validation rejects an answer. Leave blank for the default. | — |

| Column | Required | What it does | Also accepted as |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
