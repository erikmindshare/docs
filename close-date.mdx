# Close date

After the close date, the link shows "This survey is closed". Nobody can answer after that date.

<Note>
Interview Mode is in development. It is not available in installed AutoSurvey orgs yet. This page tells you what the interview does when it is available.
</Note>

## When the interview asks

The interview asks this question when your brief names an event date or a deadline.

The question is: **Close the survey on a date?** The screen selects the recommended choice first. You can click a different choice.

## The choices

| Choice | What you see | What AutoSurvey changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Close on a date** (recommended) | The survey stops taking answers after the date you pick. | No date is set. After Create, the card **Set the close date** shows. |
| **Do not close** | The survey stays open until you close it. | Nothing. |

At **Finish**, the AI can read a date in your brief and propose it as a change card. The date must be today or later. The interview keeps that date on the draft only. It does not set the close date of the survey.

## The Set up next card

| Card part | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **What and why** | You chose to close the survey after the event. Set the close date, so late answers stop. |
| **Who** | You, or an admin |
| **Link** | **Open the Send tab**. It opens the survey workspace with this survey. |
| **Done** | When the survey has a close date. |

## Change it later

Open the **Send** tab of the survey and set the close date. See [Send a survey](/workflows/send-a-survey).

## Limits

- The interview does not set a close date. The survey stays open until you set the date on the Send tab.
- The interview does not compute a date 7 days after an event.
