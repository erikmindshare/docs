## AutoSurvey in one sentence

**Build surveys, forms and applications that run inside Salesforce and write their answers straight
into it** — no integration, no export step, no second system holding your data.

## What makes it different

A survey tool bolted onto Salesforce gives you responses in someone else's database and a nightly
sync. AutoSurvey doesn't have that seam:

* **Responses are Salesforce records.** AutoSurvey saves each completed survey as records in your
  org, and copies it to a reporting object with a reporting field per question. Report on it, chart
  it, roll it up, automate off it — like any other object.
* **It runs on your infrastructure.** Surveys are served from your org, under your sharing rules
  and your security settings.
* **Respondents don't need a login.** A survey can be answered by anyone with the link, by
  community users, or only by internal staff — your choice, per survey.

## Ways to start

Click **New Survey** on the **Surveys** tab. The **Choose how to start** screen offers six ways.
These three are the most common:

  <Card title="Describe it" href="/workflows/prompt-to-flow">
    Say what you want in your own words and the AI drafts the questions and choice lists.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Start from a sample" href="/quickstart">
    Pick a ready-made survey from the sample library and change it to fit.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bring your survey" href="/workflows/import-an-existing-survey">
    Import an existing survey from Typeform, Google Forms, SurveyMonkey or GetFeedback.
  </Card>

You can also **Paste your questions**, **Import CSV/JSON**, or use **Bulk start in Google Sheet
(advanced)**. Every way ends on the same **Review** step, where you check the questions before you
create the survey. After that you edit the survey on its **Questions** tab, and you **Release** it
when it is ready. Google Sheet sync stays available as an advanced option for bulk edits — see
[Sheets to Flow](/workflows/sheets-to-flow).

## What appears in your org

Nothing is created in your schema until you **release**. Creating or importing a survey never
touches your schema.

On the first release of a survey, AutoSurvey creates:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **A reporting object** | The custom object that gets a copy of each response — one record per completed survey |
| **A reporting field per question** | So every answer is reportable without unpacking anything |
| **A page layout** | So the records are readable the moment they arrive |

Later releases only ever **add** reporting fields. They never remove one, so a report built last
quarter keeps working.

Each survey can turn its reporting object off on its **Settings** tab, in the **Data** card. AutoSurvey
still saves every response when the reporting object is off.

<Note>
  Your survey goes live the moment you release it. Reporting setup runs in the background straight
  afterwards — and AutoSurvey saves responses either way, so a survey never waits on schema to start
  collecting. See [Release and share a survey](/workflows/deploy-and-verify).
</Note>

## Where a survey runs

The same survey can be given to any of these without rebuilding it:

* **Anyone with the link** — no Salesforce login, optionally behind a CAPTCHA
* **Community or portal users** — signed in to your Experience site
* **Internal users** — inside Salesforce
