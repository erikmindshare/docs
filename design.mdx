# Design, logo and colors

A theme sets the colors, fonts and layout. The screen shows the recommended look on your real survey, and two other looks. You can pick from all 19 themes, and change any color, in Design later. For a special look, describe it in words. The AI sets only the design values that AutoSurvey has today. AutoSurvey has six fonts and no script font. If you ask for calligraphy, the AI uses Lora and tells you.

<Note>
Interview Mode is in development. It is not available in installed AutoSurvey orgs yet. This page tells you what the interview does when it is available.
</Note>

## The Choose a design step

The interview shows this step one time, after the last question, unless you turn the topic off. The survey type and the tone of your brief pick the recommended look. A look is a pair: one theme and one color palette.

The step has the title **Choose a design**. It shows three cards:

- the recommended look;
- two other looks.

Each card shows the colors of its palette and has the button **Use this design**.

The screen says: "The live preview is not on this screen yet. The colours show the palette of each design." You do not see the look on your survey in this step.

| Action | What AutoSurvey does |
| --- | --- |
| **Use this design** on a card | Selects that look. |
| Type text in the box (500 characters at most), then **Customize with AI** | The AI changes the selected look to match your words. It uses only the design values that AutoSurvey has. |
| **Keep this design** | Keeps the selected look and goes on. |
| **Skip** | Keeps the current look of the survey. No change card shows. |

At **Finish**, the look shows as a change card with the title **Design: &lt;look name&gt;**. When you used **Customize with AI**, the title ends with **, with your changes**. Before Create, Create writes the look to the survey. After Create, the look is written when you apply the card.

## The Logo and colors topic

The interview asks this question when your brief names a company or uses brand words. The question is: **Use your logo and brand colors?** The screen selects the recommended choice first. You can click a different choice.

| Choice | What you see | What AutoSurvey changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Yes, after Create** (recommended) | A Set up next card opens Design so you can add the logo. | No setting changes. After Create, the card **Add the logo** shows. |
| **No** | The survey keeps the design you picked. | Nothing. |

## The Set up next card

| Card part | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **What and why** | You chose to add a logo later. Add it on the Design page, so people see your brand. |
| **Who** | You, or an admin |
| **Link** | **Open Design**. It opens the Design page with this survey. |
| **Done** | The card has no done check. It stays in the list until you dismiss it. |

## Change it later

On the Design page you can pick from all 19 themes, change any color and add the logo. See [Themes and design](/concepts/themes-and-design).

## Limits

- The Choose a design step shows three looks only. The other themes are on the Design page.
- The step does not show a live preview of your survey.
- AutoSurvey does not take a color from your logo. You set the colors on the Design page.
