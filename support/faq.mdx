## Where do responses actually go?

Into your own Salesforce org, as records — a custom **Response Object** with a field per question,
plus a submission record and one record per answer. No sync, no export, no second database. See
[Data & Reporting](/concepts/data-and-reporting).

## Do respondents need a Salesforce login?

No. A survey can be answered by anyone with the link, by community members signed in to your
Experience site, or by internal users only — your choice, per survey.

<Tip>
  Test a public link in a **private window**. You are signed in, so the link will work for you even
  if public access isn't set up correctly — which is the most common way a launch goes wrong.
</Tip>

## Do I have to use Google Sheets?

No. Every suite is bound to a sheet, and it's the fastest way to write or bulk-edit a long survey —
but you can [describe a survey in plain language](/workflows/prompt-to-flow) instead, or
[import one you already run](/workflows/import-an-existing-survey) in Typeform, Google Forms,
SurveyMonkey or GetFeedback.

## What does publishing change in my org?

On first publish: the Response Object, a reporting field per question, and a page layout. After
that, publishing only ever **adds** fields.

## If I delete a question, does its data go away?

**No — and this catches people out.** Because publishing only adds, removing a question from your
sheet leaves its reporting field and every answer already collected exactly where they are. If you
must remove collected data, that's an ordinary Salesforce admin task. See
[What AutoSurvey Creates in Your Org](/admin/objects-permissions-layouts).

## Can I change a survey that's already collecting responses?

Yes. Reword questions, change scales, add new ones. What you must not do is change a question's
**API Name** — that creates a *second* question with a second field rather than renaming the first.
See [Questions, Versions & Releases](/concepts/question-library-and-versioning).

## Can a question depend on an answer from an earlier page?

Yes. A **Show If** rule can use any answer the respondent has already given. The only thing it can't
do is depend on an answer they haven't reached yet. See
[Conditional Visibility](/concepts/conditional-visibility).

## Is any of my data sent to an AI provider?

Only the **survey design content you write**, and only if you use the optional AI authoring
features. **Respondent answers are never sent to an AI service.** Analytics and export run entirely
inside your org. See [Security & Privacy](/admin/security-and-privacy).

## Does AutoSurvey have an API?

It doesn't need one — your responses are Salesforce records, so you integrate with Salesforce. See
[Integrating with AutoSurvey](/api/overview).

## What happens if we uninstall?

AutoSurvey's own objects and the responses in them are removed, as with any managed package. Your
**customer-owned reporting object and its data remain**, carrying responder identity, timestamps and
the full answer set. **Export anything you want to keep before uninstalling.**
