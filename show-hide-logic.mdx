# Show and hide questions

Show and hide logic shows a question only when an earlier answer matches. For example, "Why not?" shows only after the answer No.

<Note>
Interview Mode is in development. It is not available in installed AutoSurvey orgs yet. This page tells you what the interview does when it is available.
</Note>

## When the interview asks

The interview asks this question when your brief says that different answers need different questions, for example "if they say no, ask why".

The question is: **Show some questions only after some answers?** The screen selects the recommended choice first. You can click a different choice.

## The choices

| Choice | What you see | What AutoSurvey changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Yes, add the logic from my brief** (recommended) | AutoSurvey proposes change cards for the logic. | No setting changes. The answer goes to the AI at **Finish**. |
| **No** | Every person sees every question. | Nothing. |

This topic uses the AI at **Finish**. The AI gets your answer and its result text. It can reword, remove or add questions, change the answer options and change a question type. Each proposal is a change card that you keep or skip. If the AI proposes nothing, no change card shows.

## Limits

- The AI cannot add a show or hide rule. It can add a follow-up question, for example "Why not?", but that question shows to every person.
- Add each rule yourself in the survey builder after Create. See [Conditional visibility](/concepts/conditional-visibility).
