# Thank-you page and redirect

The thank-you page shows after Submit. You can add a short text and a link. Or you can send the person to a web page instead.

<Note>
Interview Mode is in development. It is not available in installed AutoSurvey orgs yet. This page tells you what the interview does when it is available.
</Note>

## When the interview asks

The interview asks this question when your brief names a website, a reward or a next action for people to take.

The question is: **What happens after people press Submit?** The screen selects the recommended choice first. You can click a different choice.

## The choices

| Choice | What you see | What AutoSurvey changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Use the default text** (recommended) | AutoSurvey writes a short welcome and thank-you. | Nothing. The survey keeps its current texts. |
| **Write my own text** | AutoSurvey writes text that fits your brief. You can change it. | At **Finish**, the AI can propose a welcome text and a thank-you text. Each proposal is a change card. |
| **Send people to a page** | People go to the page you name when they finish. A Set up next card opens Design, where you type the address. | No setting changes. After Create, the card **Type the finish page address** shows. |

This topic uses the AI at **Finish**. A text from the AI is plain text with 2,000 characters at most. If the AI proposes no text, no change card shows.

## The Set up next card

The choice **Send people to a page** adds this card.

| Card part | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **What and why** | You chose to send people to a page when they finish. On the Design page, open the ending and type the address. |
| **Who** | You, or an admin |
| **Link** | **Open Design**. It opens the Design page with this survey. |
| **Done** | When the survey has a redirect address. |

## Change it later

On the Design page, open the ending. There you set the **Message**, the **Redirect URL** and the **Redirect link label**. A redirect address starts with http:// or https:// and has 255 characters at most. See [Themes and design](/concepts/themes-and-design).

## Limits

- **Use the default text** does not write a text. The survey keeps the texts that it has.
- **Write my own text** depends on the AI. The AI can return no text.
- The interview does not ask for the redirect address. You type it on the Design page.
