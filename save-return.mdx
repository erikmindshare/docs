---
title: "Save and come back"
description: "Who can stop in the middle of a survey and continue later, and who must finish in one visit."
---

Save and come back lets a person stop and finish later. It is part of the org feature Respondent
self-service, which an admin turns on in AutoSurvey Setup. When the feature is on, each survey has
it, and you can turn it off for one survey on its Settings tab.

A long survey can take more than one visit. The way that the person opens the survey decides if the
person can continue later.

| How the person opens the survey | Can continue later |
| --- | --- |
| Signed in to your org or to your Experience site | Yes. The survey finds the draft and offers to continue. |
| A personal invitation link | Yes. The same link opens the survey where the person stopped. |
| The public link, with no sign-in | No. Each visit starts a new response. |

To let people continue later, send the survey by invitation or ask them to sign in.
