description: "Ask one short follow-up question after an open-text answer. Turn on the feature, then turn on follow-ups for each question that needs them."
---

A respondent writes "It was hard." You do not know what was hard. With **AI follow-up questions**,
the survey asks one short question after an open-text answer, for example "What made it hard?".

## Before you start

Two switches in **Setup**, **Optional Capabilities** must be on. The two switches are off until an
admin turns them on.

1. **AI reads answers**. This switch lets AutoSurvey send the text of survey answers to your AI
   provider. While it is off, no answer text goes to the AI provider.
2. **AI follow-up questions**. You cannot turn it on while **AI reads answers** is off.

## Turn on follow-ups for a question

Follow-ups are a setting of each text or long-text question. Open the survey, open the question, and find the group
**Follow-up questions** in the display properties.

| Property | Meaning | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ask follow-ups** | After an open-text answer, the survey asks one short follow-up question. | no follow-up question |
| **Most follow-ups on this question** | How many follow-up questions one answer can get: **1** or **2**. | 1 |
| **Goal of the follow-ups** | What you want to learn from the follow-ups. The AI reads it when it writes a question. 120 characters at most. | no goal is set |
| **Follow-up questions from your list** | One question of your own for each kind of answer. Leave a box blank to keep the standard question. Each question has 200 characters at most. | the standard questions |
| **Never ask about** | Words or topics, separated by `\|`. A follow-up question that names one of them is not asked. | no topic is blocked |
| **Also ask after a low score on** | An NPS question. When its score is 0 to 6, the follow-up shows also after a long answer. | only the answer decides |

The other five properties apply only when **Ask follow-ups** is on.

## Limits

- A follow-up question that you write has no `;` in it. Use a comma.
- **Ask follow-ups** works only while **AI follow-up questions** is on in Setup.
