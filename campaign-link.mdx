# Campaign link

Each person who answers becomes a campaign member with the status "Answered". Campaign reports then show who answered.

<Note>
Interview Mode is in development. It is not available in installed AutoSurvey orgs yet. This page tells you what the interview does when it is available.
</Note>

## When the interview asks

The interview asks this question when your brief uses the word campaign, or names a webinar or a marketing event.

The question is: **Link the answers to a Salesforce campaign?** The screen selects the recommended choice first. You can click a different choice.

## The choices

| Choice | What you see | What AutoSurvey changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Yes, link to a campaign** (recommended) | A Set up next card opens Campaigns. On the campaign, add this survey under Campaign Surveys. | No setting changes. After Create, the card **Add the survey to a campaign** shows. |
| **No** | The survey stays separate from campaigns. | Nothing. |

## The Set up next card

| Card part | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **What and why** | You chose to link this survey to a campaign. Open the campaign, then add the survey under Campaign Surveys. |
| **Who** | You, or an admin |
| **Link** | **Open Campaigns**. It opens the Campaign list. It does not take the survey. Open the campaign that you need. |
| **Done** | When a campaign has this survey under Campaign Surveys. |

## What the campaign link does

When a person who is a campaign member answers, AutoSurvey sets the member status to **Responded**. See [Campaign distribution](/admin/campaign-distribution).

## Limits

- The interview does not pick a campaign. You add the survey on the campaign.
- The member status is **Responded**, not "Answered".
