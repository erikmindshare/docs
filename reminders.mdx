# Reminders

A reminder goes only to people who did not answer. A person who answered gets no reminder.

<Note>
Interview Mode is in development. It is not available in installed AutoSurvey orgs yet. This page tells you what the interview does when it is available.
</Note>

## When the interview asks

The interview asks this question when you send the survey to a known list by email or by text message.

The question is: **Remind the people who did not answer?** The screen selects the recommended choice first. You can click a different choice.

## The choices

| Choice | What you see | What AutoSurvey changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **One reminder, day 3** (recommended) | People who did not answer get one reminder on day 3. | Create makes a reminder schedule: one reminder, on day 3. |
| **Two reminders, day 3 and day 7** | People who did not answer get a reminder on day 3 and on day 7. | Create makes a reminder schedule: two reminders, on day 3 and on day 7. |
| **No reminders** | People get the survey once. | Nothing. |

Create makes the reminder schedule when your account can create and change reminder schedules. Create does not make a schedule in these cases, and the card **Set the reminders** shows. You then check the schedule yourself:

- Your account cannot change reminder schedules.
- The survey has a reminder schedule that is on with other values.
- The survey has more than one reminder schedule.
- The reminder schedule of the survey sends text messages.

## The Set up next card

| Card part | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **What and why** | You chose reminders. Set the reminder schedule, so people who did not answer get a reminder. |
| **Who** | You, or an admin |
| **Link** | **Open the Send tab**. It opens the survey workspace with this survey. |
| **Done** | When the survey has an active reminder schedule. |

## Change it later

Open the **Send** tab of the survey. Use the follow-up reminders line. See [Send a survey](/workflows/send-a-survey).

## Limits

- A reminder schedule is not enough to send email. An admin must also turn on **Send reminder email for real** in AutoSurvey Settings, and the user that runs the scheduled job needs the Reminders Live permission. Until then, AutoSurvey logs each reminder as a dry run and sends no email. The card tells you when the schedule cannot send yet.
