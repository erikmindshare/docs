# Reminders

A reminder goes only to people who did not answer. A person who answered gets no reminder.

<Note>
Interview Mode is in development. It is not available in installed AutoSurvey orgs yet. This page tells you what the interview does when it is available.
</Note>

## When the interview asks

The interview asks this question when you send the survey to a known list by email or by text message.

The question is: **Remind the people who did not answer?** The screen selects the recommended choice first. You can click a different choice.

## The choices

| Choice | What you see | What AutoSurvey changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **One reminder, day 3** (recommended) | People who did not answer get one reminder on day 3. | Keeps the value 1 on the draft. After Create, the card **Set the reminders** shows. |
| **Two reminders, day 3 and day 7** | People who did not answer get a reminder on day 3 and on day 7. | Keeps the value 2 on the draft. After Create, the card **Set the reminders** shows. |
| **No reminders** | People get the survey once. | Nothing. |

The interview does not make a reminder schedule. The days 3 and 7 are a suggestion. You set the schedule yourself.

## The Set up next card

| Card part | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **What and why** | You chose reminders. Set the reminder schedule, so people who did not answer get a reminder. |
| **Who** | You, or an admin |
| **Link** | **Open the Send tab**. It opens the survey workspace with this survey. |
| **Done** | When the survey has an active reminder schedule. |

## Change it later

Open the **Send** tab of the survey. Use the follow-up reminders line. See [Send a survey](/workflows/send-a-survey).

## Limits

- No reminder goes out until you set the schedule on the Send tab.
