title: "Send a Survey"
description: "Use the survey's Send tab to see who can respond, share the public link or QR code, give in-app access, and email people."
---

The **Send** tab of a survey is where you get the survey to respondents. It shows who can respond
now, the state of each channel, and the sends you made.

You must release the survey before you can send it. To release, see
[Release & Share](/workflows/deploy-and-verify).

## Who can respond

The block at the top of the tab says who can answer the survey now.

* Before the first release, it says: *Nobody yet. The survey has no released version.*
* After a release, it shows the version (for example *Version 2 is released · Accepting
  responses*) and one sentence about reach now. For example: *Now: anyone with the public link can
  respond.* If the public link is paused or not ready, the sentence says that only people you send
  the survey to can respond.
* When collection is closed, it says that links show a closed message, and that a response in
  progress can finish. A link sends you to Results to reopen collection.
* When email is not set up, it says *Email is not set up* and links to the **Email** row.

### When a control is off

When **＋ New send ▾** is off, the reason shows as text next to the button, not in a tooltip. Before
the first release it says *Release version 1 first. Then you can send.* On a closed survey it says
*Collection is closed. Reopen collection to send.*, or that you need permission to reopen.

## Help me choose, or All channels

After the first release, two tabs show. **Help me choose** asks *How should I send this?* and
recommends a channel. If that channel is not ready, it tells you to fix it first. **All channels**
shows the channel rows. The Send tab remembers your choice.

## The channel rows

The **Channels:** line gives a short summary, for example *All 4 channels ready*. Click it to show
or hide the rows. **Check again** reads the state of every channel again.

Each row has a status chip:

| Chip | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** / **Ready** | The channel works. |
| **Needs attention** | A channel that already collected responses has a problem. |
| **Not set up** | The channel is not set up. A channel that never collected a response shows this, not **Needs attention**. |
| **Partly set up** | The channel works, but a setup step is not done. |
| **Paused** | You paused the public link. |
| **Closed** | Collection for the survey is closed. |

### Public link

The **Public link** row is for people with no Salesforce login. Anyone with the link can respond
without signing in. The link runs on an Experience site in your org, so the org needs a guest site
first. If the link is not ready, the row shows **Set up…** and, when you open it, **What’s missing**
with each check that is not done. See [Setup Home Guide](/admin/setup).

When the link is ready, the row gives you:

* **Copy link**, and **QR ▾** with a preview of the code, **Download PNG** and **Copy image**.

Open the row (the **▾** chevron) to see more:

* **Who can respond** — **Anyone with the link**, or **Invitation required**. If you have
  permission, **Require an invitation…** or **Allow anyone with the link…** changes it.
* **Start at** — the link opens at a question you choose, not at the start. **Beginning** is the
  default. With a question chosen, the link opens on the screen of that question and hides
  **Previous** there. Required questions before it are still asked before the survey can be
  submitted.
* The full link, and an **Embed** snippet with **Copy snippet**. The QR code and the snippet use
  the same link as **Copy link**, so they follow **Start at** too.
* **Pause public link…** — a new visitor sees a paused message and cannot start. Anyone already
  answering can finish. The link and QR code stay valid. **Resume public link…** turns it on again.

The release dialog has a **Public link** view too: it says what the link is, whether it works now,
and what to do next.

### In-app

The **In-app** row is for people in your Salesforce org. They take the survey inside the app.
The count reads, for example, *3 with access*: the people who hold the permission set of the
survey. Releasing gives that access to the person who releases.

**Open in app** opens the survey. Open the row for **Copy internal link** and the setup checks.
If a check fails, **Fix →** shows on the row.

### Site members

The **Site members** row is for people who sign in to your Experience site, for example a partner
portal. Without a site, the row says *Needs an Experience site.* Open the row for the setup steps
under **Set up member access**. When all steps are done, the row gives the members link with
**Copy link**, and **Assign members…** adds people.

### Email

The **Email** row covers list and campaign sends. You start them from **＋ New send ▾**.
AutoSurvey sends email only from a **verified send-from address** (an org-wide email address that
Salesforce verified). If there is none, the row shows **Set up email**. It opens the email setup
panel in the row. **Hide setup** closes it.

## Set up email

The **Set up email invitations** panel has two tasks:

1. **Add a verified send-from address** — the steps, and **Open Org-Wide Email Addresses in Setup**.
2. **Verify your email domain with DKIM** — the steps, and **Open DKIM Keys in Setup**. The panel
   does not show this task when it is done. AutoSurvey can check the DKIM key. It cannot check SPF
   and DMARC.

**Check again** reads the email setup again. When the panel finds a verified address, the chip on
the Email row changes with no page reload. You need permission to change Setup. If you do not have
it, send the steps to your Salesforce administrator.

<Warning>
  Email also needs the **survey base URL**, set on Setup Home in the **Public Survey Access**
  section. Without it, an email invitation has no survey link. **Help me choose** blocks email until
  the base URL is set.
</Warning>

## New send

**＋ New send ▾** is the one way to start a send. The first line of the menu says what you send,
for example *Sends version 2 · Accepting responses*. The menu has:

* **Email people…** — pick Leads, Contacts or Users, or paste addresses. No campaign is needed.
* **Email a campaign…** — send to the members of a Salesforce campaign. See
  [Campaign Survey Distribution](/admin/campaign-distribution).
* **Assign in-app…** — no email. The survey shows in the Salesforce app of the people you assign.

Each send makes a tracked round in **Send activity**.

## Email people

The **Email people** dialog sends a survey invitation to each person.

1. Use **Find people** or **Paste addresses** to add recipients.
2. Click **Preview recipients**.
3. Write the **Subject** and the **Body**.
4. Choose the **From** address.
5. Click **Send me a test**. AutoSurvey sends this email, from this address, to you only. Its link
   opens a test run, which does not count in results.
6. Click **Send**, then **Confirm & send**.

If there is no verified send-from address, the dialog says so when it opens: *This email cannot be
sent yet: there is no verified send-from address.* You can still pick people. **Set up email**
shows the email setup panel in the dialog. **Back** returns to your picked people and your message.

If the **From** address is not verified, or its domain has no active DKIM key, the dialog says so
when you choose it.

After the send, the dialog says **Sending started**.

## Send activity

After the first send, **Send activity** shows the last send, the **Accepted** and **Failed**
counts, and **Responses**. **See responses in Results →** opens the Results tab.

Below it, the **Sends**, **People** and **Registrations** tabs list the detail. The **People** tab
lists email invitations (list, campaign or flow) and public-link registrations. In-app access does
not make an invitation, so in-app people are not on the People tab. The **In-app** row counts them.

## Follow-up reminders

The **Follow-up reminders:** line shows **On** or **Off**. When reminders are on, it shows
**Edit schedule…** and **Turn off**. When they are off, it shows **Turn on**. When live sending is off, it shows **Dry-run mode**: reminders run but send no email.
See [Nudging people who haven't answered](/workflows/deploy-and-verify#nudging-people-who-havent-answered).

## When the survey is closed

When collection is closed, the **Public link**, **In-app** and **Site members** rows show **Closed**
in place of **Active**. A row with a problem keeps its own chip. **＋ New send ▾** is off.
The reminders line does not show. To open the survey again, see
[Stopping collection](/workflows/deploy-and-verify#stopping-collection).
