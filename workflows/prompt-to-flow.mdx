The fastest way to a first draft: describe the survey in plain language and let AutoSurvey write the
questions and choice lists. What comes back is an ordinary AutoSurvey survey — you edit, validate and
publish it exactly like one you wrote by hand.

## Where it lives

Open your suite in **Suite Manager** and go to **Populate Survey Questions** (step 5), or pick
**Populate Questions...** from the **⋯** menu in the suite header to jump straight there.

That step offers six ways to add questions — three under **Start with AI**, three under
**Bring your own** — and you can mix them:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Freeform prompt** | Describe the survey; AutoSurvey generates questions and option sets |
| **Guided** | Answer a few quick questions and AutoSurvey drafts it |
| **Paste your questions** | Your wording; AutoSurvey detects the types and options |
| **Bring your survey** | Migrate an existing survey word-for-word — see [Import an Existing Survey](/workflows/import-an-existing-survey) |
| **Import CSV/JSON** | Paste or upload CSV or JSON |
| **Edit in Google Sheets** | Open the Google Sheet and write them yourself |

## Two ways to describe what you want

**Freeform prompt** — one box, free text. Write what you'd tell a colleague.

**Guided** — separate fields for the topic, the audience, and the key decision or goal the survey
serves. Worth the extra minute when the survey has to earn its place: naming the *decision*
is what stops a generator producing twenty interesting questions that answer nothing.

### A worked example

> We run a three-day developer conference. I want a post-event survey for attendees that tells us
> whether to run it again in the same format. Cover the talks, the venue, the food and the
> networking. Keep it under five minutes. Include one NPS question and a free-text "what should we
> change" at the end.

That produces a screen-structured draft with an NPS question, rating questions per track, and a
closing long-text — with the choice lists built as reusable [Option Sets](/concepts/option-sets).

## Fast or Standard

Under **Optional AI Guidance** you can choose how hard AutoSurvey works:

| Mode | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Fast** | One pass. Quicker, and usually needs more cleanup |
| **Standard** | Two passes — the first designs the question set and screen structure, the second compiles it for consistency |

**Standard is the right default for most surveys.** Use Fast when you're exploring shapes rather
than drafting the real thing.

## What happens to the result

The generated questions and option sets are imported into your suite's bound Google Sheet. From
there it is an ordinary survey: edit anything you like, validate, import, publish. Nothing goes live
because it was generated.

<Tip>
  Treat the first generation as a draft, not an answer. The fastest workflow is generate → skim in
  the sheet → delete a third → publish.
</Tip>

## Large surveys

AutoSurvey generates up to **500 questions** in one go.

Anything over about 150 questions is automatically moved to a background job — you don't have to do
anything, the progress panel says it's working in the background, and you get a notification when
it's done. Very large surveys need a higher AI tier:

| Survey size | Runs | Tier needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up to ~120 questions | Immediately | Any |
| ~120–350 questions | In the background | Advanced or Premium |
| ~350–500 questions | In the background | Premium |

## Before your first generation

<Warning>
  **AI generation needs the AI model catalog seeded first.** If it hasn't been, the AI options in
  step 5 are disabled and the panel shows an **AI generation is not available** warning explaining
  why. The fix is the **Seed AI models** button in Setup — running setup again does *not* seed it.
  See the [Setup Home Guide](/admin/setup).
</Warning>
