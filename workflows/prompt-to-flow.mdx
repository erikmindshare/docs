The fastest way to a first draft: describe the survey in plain language and let AutoSurvey write the
questions and choice lists. You review the draft before the survey is created. What comes back is an
ordinary AutoSurvey survey — you edit and release it exactly like one you wrote by hand.

## Where it lives

Click **New Survey**. The dialog opens on **Choose how to start**, with six ways as cards.
**Describe it** is the first card, and the dialog opens on it:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Describe it** | Your own words. The AI drafts the questions and the answer options |
| **Paste your questions** | Your wording. AutoSurvey suggests the answer types |
| **Start from a sample** | Open a sample to see what it asks, then click **Use this sample** |
| **Import CSV/JSON** | Paste or upload a questions table, and the answer options if you have them |
| **Bring your survey** | Copy an existing survey word for word — see [Import an Existing Survey](/workflows/import-an-existing-survey) |
| **Bulk start in Google Sheet (advanced)** | Creates the survey first, with a blank sheet — see [Sheets to Survey](/workflows/sheets-to-flow) |

Every way except the Google Sheet way goes to a **Review** screen first. No survey is created until
you click **Create survey** on the Review screen.

<Note>
  **The survey already exists?** Open it and pick **Populate questions…** (a survey with no
  questions) or **Change questions…** from the **⋯** menu in the survey header. On the
  **Questions** tab you can also click **Replace all questions…**. These show the same kinds of
  ways: **Describe it**, **Answer a few questions**, **Paste your questions**, **Import CSV/JSON**,
  **Bring your survey** and **Bulk edit in Google Sheet (advanced)**. If the survey has questions,
  AutoSurvey asks before it replaces them.
</Note>

## Two ways to describe what you want

**Describe it** — one box, free text. Write what you would tell a colleague.

**Answer guided questions** — click it under the box to open five short questions. The AI writes
the survey from your answers:

| Question | What you choose |
| --- | --- |
| **What kind of survey is this?** | **General**, **Customer satisfaction**, **Net Promoter Score (NPS)**, **Event or community** or **Onboarding**. Each type says what it is for |
| **Who answers it?** | The audience, in your words |
| **What decision does it inform?** | The decision the answers serve |
| **How long?** | **Short** (5 to 8 questions, about 2 min), **Medium** (8 to 12 questions, 2 to 3 min) or **Long** (12 or more questions, 3 min or more) |
| **Tone** | **Neutral**, **Warm and friendly**, **Formal** or **Playful**, or type your own. Each preset shows a sample question |

You can use the box, the guided questions, or both. The guided questions are worth the extra
minute when the survey has to earn its place: naming the *decision* is what stops a generator
from writing twenty interesting questions that answer nothing.

### A worked example

> We run a three-day developer conference. I want a post-event survey for attendees that tells us
> whether to run it again in the same format. Cover the talks, the venue, the food and the
> networking. Keep it under five minutes. Include one NPS question and a free-text "what should we
> change" at the end.

That produces a draft with screens, an NPS question, rating questions per track, and a closing
long-text question. The choice lists are built as reusable [Option Sets](/concepts/option-sets).

## The AI level

Beside **Build my survey**, the line **AI:** and a tier name shows the AI tier this build uses. It starts
at your own tier. Click **Change AI level** to pick another tier that your org offers. Each tier
lists its models.

* **Custom** — shows only if you have the **Choose AI Models** permission. You pick the model for
  each step, for this survey only. The next survey starts from your tier again.
* **Manage tiers and models in Setup** — shows only if you can change the tiers.

<Note>
  The **Populate questions…** / **Change questions…** path for an existing survey has its own
  **AI Tier** list, and a **Generation Quality** choice under **Optional AI Guidance**:
  **Fast (Quick draft)** uses one AI pass and can need more cleanup; **Standard (Higher accuracy)**
  uses more passes for better structure and consistency. Standard is the right default for most
  surveys. The New Survey flow always uses Standard.
</Note>

## What happens to the result

Click **Build my survey**. The working screen says it usually takes under a minute; some runs take
up to 2 minutes. Then **Review your survey** opens. Nothing goes live because it was generated.

On the Review screen:

* **List** shows each question with its type. **Preview** shows each screen as a read-only form.
* **Why these questions** quotes your description and answers. **Change my description** takes you
  back.
* Click **×** to remove a question. **Undo** puts it back, newest first.
* If a row has a value AutoSurvey cannot accept, the row shows **Blocks Create**, and the message
  above the list has **Show the row**. Change it, then build again.
* **Title**, **Workspace** and **Survey ID** sit together at the top. A title that another survey
  already uses gives a warning with **Rename** or **Keep it**; it does not block Create. The Survey
  ID is part of your public link and cannot change after you create the survey.
* **Look** — choose a theme, then its palette. A sample question shows the chosen look. This is
  optional; you can change it later on the Design page.
* **Advanced** — a **Description**, and **Where answers are stored** (the Salesforce object that
  holds the answers). Both have defaults.

Click **Create survey**. AutoSurvey creates the survey and its Google Sheet, and opens the
**Questions** tab in Preview. From there it is an ordinary survey: edit, add and move questions on
the Questions tab, then release it.

<Tip>
  Treat the first generation as a draft, not an answer. The fastest workflow is build → remove a
  third on the Review screen → create → change the rest on the Questions tab.
</Tip>

## Large surveys

AutoSurvey generates up to **500 questions** in one go.

Anything over about 150 questions is automatically moved to a background job — you don't have to do
anything, the progress panel says it's working in the background, and you get a notification when
it's done. Very large surveys need a higher AI tier:

| Survey size | Runs | Tier needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up to ~120 questions | Immediately | Any |
| ~120–350 questions | In the background | Advanced or Premium |
| ~350–500 questions | In the background | Premium |

## Before your first generation

<Warning>
  **AI generation needs the AI model catalog seeded first.** If it hasn't been, the AI ways under
  **Populate questions…** / **Change questions…** are disabled and show an **AI generation is not
  available** warning that explains why. The fix is the **Seed AI models** button in Setup — running setup again does *not* seed it.
  See the [Setup Home Guide](/admin/setup).
</Warning>
