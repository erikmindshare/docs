# Rewards

People who finish get a reward, for example a gift card code. A reward needs a bot check, so people cannot claim it many times.

<Note>
Interview Mode is in development. It is not available in installed AutoSurvey orgs yet. This page tells you what the interview does when it is available.
</Note>

## Before you start: Shield Rewards

The reward feature is **Shield Rewards**. It is off by default, and it needs the **Fraud & Quality Shield** feature to be on too. While Shield Rewards is off in your org, the interview shows the Rewards topic as turned off. You can turn the feature on in AutoSurvey Setup, ask your admin, or click **Continue without it for now**.

## When the interview asks

The interview asks this question when the survey goes to strangers or a panel, when your brief names an incentive, or when the survey takes more than 10 minutes. The question is: **Offer a reward for answering?** The topic has no pre-selection. You must click a choice.

## The choices

| Choice | What you see | What AutoSurvey changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No** (recommended) | People answer with no reward. | Nothing. |
| **Yes, offer a reward** | A Set up next card opens Shield Rewards with this survey picked. | No survey setting. After Create, the **Reward for answers** card shows. |

The interview does not set up the reward. You set it up in Shield Rewards.

## The Set up next card

| Card part | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | Reward for answers |
| **What and why** | You chose to offer a reward. Shield Rewards pays people for a complete answer after an approval. |
| **Who** | An admin |
| **Link** | **Open Shield Rewards**. This page does not open a survey. On that page, pick your survey. |
| **Done** | When the survey has an enabled Shield policy binding with the reward turned on. |

While Shield Rewards is off, the card shows the state **Feature is off**. When the binding exists, the card shows **Done**. **Dismiss** and **Don't suggest this again** take the card off the list for this survey.
