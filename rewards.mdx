title: "Shield Rewards"
description: "Pay a respondent a reward after the Fraud & Quality Shield accepts the response. Set up the Tremendous connection, a budget and a reward rule."

A reward pays people for a complete answer. It helps when you ask strangers or a long survey. The
reward needs payment approval. The first time, a short setup runs.

A reward brings more answers, but it also brings people who answer many times to get paid. **Shield
Rewards** pays a reward only after the **Fraud & Quality Shield** accepts the response. AutoSurvey
sends each approved reward to Tremendous, and Tremendous delivers it.

## Before you start

- **Fraud & Quality Shield** is on. Shield Rewards cannot be on without it. See [Bot check and
  Shield](/bot-check).
- An admin turns on the card **Shield Rewards** in **Setup**, **Optional Capabilities**. The feature
  is off until an admin turns it on.
- You have a Tremendous account.
- The reward roles are permission sets: **AutoSurvey Rewards Manager**, **AutoSurvey Rewards
  Approver**, **AutoSurvey Rewards Corrector** and **AutoSurvey Rewards Worker**. The switch gives
  **AutoSurvey Rewards Manager** to the AutoSurvey admins. An admin assigns the other sets.

## 1. Set up rewards

Open the tab **Shield Settings** and go to **Rewards**. **Set up rewards** takes you through the steps.
Payments stay stopped until you resume them. Nothing pays while you set up.

1. Ask your Salesforce admin to put your Tremendous API key in the Tremendous external credential. Do
   not paste the key in a survey, an email or a chat. Then click **Test the connection**.
2. Choose **How Tremendous identifies a recipient**.
3. In Tremendous, turn on W-9 collection. Then confirm it here. Tremendous collects the form.
   AutoSurvey does not store tax data.
4. Choose the **Org default for new reward rules**:
   - **Require approval for every payment**
   - **Pay automatically up to an amount**
   - **Pay automatically after response approval**

   You need the permission Rewards Approve Spend to change this mode.
5. Add a budget.

## 2. Add a budget

A budget is a spending allowance. It is not your Tremendous balance. If you raise a cap, no money
goes to Tremendous.

1. In **Budgets**, on the tab **Shield Settings**, click **New budget**.
2. Enter the **Name**, the **Cap**, the **Start** date and the **End** date.
3. Enter **Alert at %**.
4. Click **Review changes**, then save.

Add a budget before you turn on a reward rule.

## 3. Make a reward rule

A rule belongs to a form or a survey that Shield protects. In **Reward rules**, click **Edit** on the
row of the form or survey.

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Reward amount (USD)** | The amount for one accepted response. |
| **Tremendous campaign id** | The Tremendous campaign that delivers the reward. Click **Check campaign**. |
| **Budget** | The budget that pays for this rule. |
| **US residence question** | The survey question that says if the respondent lives in the US. |
| **Approval** | How a payment gets approved. |
| **Approve automatically up to (USD)** | AutoSurvey approves a payment up to this amount with no person. |
| **Message to the respondent** | The text that goes with the reward. 200 characters at most. |
| **Notice text before the survey (optional)** | The text that the respondent reads before the survey. |

Under **Cost**, enter the **Expected eligible responses**. The default is the count of responses in
the last 30 days. Read the list **Before you activate**. Click **Save draft** to keep your changes. Click **Activate
offer**, with the version number, to start the rule.

To stop a rule, click **Turn off rewards**.

## Stop all payments

On the tab **Shield Settings**, in **Emergency stop**, click **Stop payments**. You can give a reason. AutoSurvey sends no reward
while payments are stopped.

To start again, select "I checked why payments stopped. Approved rewards will go to Tremendous." and
click **Resume payments**.

## The rewards list

The tab **Shield Rewards** lists each reward. Click **Open** to see one reward. You can select more than one
reward. Some actions on the selected rewards ask for a **Written reason** before you click **Confirm**.

The dashboard shows **Now · needs attention**, **Now · money** and **Shield and rewards for a
period**.
