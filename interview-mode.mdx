---
title: Interview Mode
description: Interview Mode asks you questions about a new survey, and each answer changes the questions or a setting. This page tells you why you use it, who can use it and how to turn it on.
---

A first AI survey from one paragraph is a guess. The AI does not know who gets the survey, what you do with a low score, or which words your company does not use. You then change the survey by hand, one question and one setting at a time.

Interview Mode removes that work. After the AI writes the first survey, it asks you about the things that it could not know. Each answer changes the questions or a setting. You see each change, and you can undo it.

## An example

Harbor Community Health wants a survey after each clinic visit. Jordan Rivera writes this brief:

> After a clinic visit, ask the patient how it went. I want to learn if the wait was acceptable and if the nurse explained the next steps. Reach the nurse manager when a score is low.

The AI writes the survey. Then it asks Jordan, for example:

- Who follows up on a low score: the survey owner, a user, a queue, or the owner of the Contact?
- Do people get a reminder after three days?
- Which look does the survey use?

Jordan answers the questions and clicks **Update the survey**. The change list shows each question that changed. Jordan clicks **Undo** on one change, and then creates the survey.

## What the AI does, and what you decide

| The AI | You |
| --- | --- |
| Reads your brief and the survey. | Write the brief. |
| Selects the questions to ask you. It asks only about the things that your survey needs. | Answer, or click **Skip**. You can stop at each step. |
| Recommends a choice on many screens. | Select the choice. A recommended choice is a suggestion only. |
| Writes the changes to the questions when you click **Update the survey**. | Read the change list. Click **Undo** on a change that you do not want. |
| Recommends a look for the survey. | Select the look, or keep the standard look. |

The interview ends when you click **Create survey**. You release the survey and send it yourself.

## Who can use it

You see the interview only when all of these are true:

- You have author access and edit access to surveys.
- The feature **Change with AI** is on in the org.
- You have the permission set **AutoSurvey_AI_Refine**.
- The AI model of the org can run the interview.

You can use the interview only on a survey draft that you own.

## Turn it on

**Change with AI** is on in a new installation. In a new installation, AutoSurvey gives the permission set **AutoSurvey_AI_Refine** to the full AutoSurvey admins only. The survey authors get it when an admin turns **Change with AI** on in Setup Home.

If the feature is off, the survey shows this message: "AI help to refine this survey is off in this org." To turn it on:

1. Open **AutoSurvey Setup**.
2. Go to **Features**.
3. Set **Change with AI** to **On**.

Only an AutoSurvey admin can change this switch. Each AI request of an author uses the AI allowance of the org. See [What it costs, limits and privacy](/interview/cost-limits-privacy).

### Two settings for the admin

| Setting | Where | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Default interview depth** | **AutoSurvey Setup**, **Features**, the card **Interview depth** | Sets how many questions the interview asks about a new survey. It does not change the AI model. An author can change the depth for one survey. |
| **Who helps survey authors** | **AutoSurvey Setup** | The interview tells an author who to ask when a feature is off, or when the account cannot do a step. Type a **Contact name or team**, for example "the Salesforce team". Do not type an email address. If you leave it blank, the interview shows the names of AutoSurvey admins. Select **Do not show names of AutoSurvey admins** to hide the names. |

## Questions from your brief

The AI reads your brief and the survey. It can then ask a few questions about what your brief says. These are the questions from your brief. They are not feature settings. Your answer helps the AI when it updates the survey.

Example: your brief says "reach the nurse manager". The AI can ask who gets a low score.

- The choices are written by the AI for your brief. For some questions you can select more than one choice.
- **No, I do not want this** tells the AI not to add that thing to the survey.
- **Skip** decides later. The AI does not use the question now.
- Some questions ask for one fact that only you know, for example the address of a page.

The number of these questions depends on the depth. See [The three depths](/interview/start#the-three-depths).

## The list of steps

The list beside the screens shows each step of the interview and a mark for each answer. Click a step to change its answer. The list also has **Update now**, **Overall instructions**, **Build it with what I told you** and **Go to the end →**.

For each mark and each control, see [The list of steps](/interview/answer-update-undo#the-list-of-steps).

## Ask more questions

If the AI rates your survey complex, the interview can offer more questions. The panel gives the reasons, for example many groups of people.

- **Yes, ask more**: the AI asks up to 3 more questions now. This is one AI call.
- **No, keep it as it is**: the interview continues with the questions that it has.

You can also click **Ask me more** on the last screen of the interview.

## Premium for this survey

If the AI rates your survey complex, the interview can offer the Premium AI level for this one survey. The panel gives the reasons. When AutoSurvey has a cost estimate, the panel shows the cost of each AI run.

- **Yes, use Premium**: a stronger AI model makes the interview plan, the changes and any rebuild of this survey. Only this survey uses it.
- **No, keep my tier**: the interview continues with the AI model of your current AI level.

## Survey basics

The interview can ask four basic questions. The AI reads the answers from your brief, and you correct them if they are wrong.

| Question | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Survey type** | The guidance that the AI uses for the questions. |
| **Who answers it** | The questions and the tone fit this group of people. |
| **Length** | How many questions the survey has. A shorter survey gets more answers. |
| **Tone** | How the questions sound, for example friendly or professional. Each tone shows a sample question. |

## Words to avoid

Some surveys must not use some words, or must not ask about some subjects. The interview can ask you for them. You select **Avoid the words or topics I select** or **Nothing to avoid**.

AutoSurvey then does not use these words and does not ask about these subjects, when it writes the survey and each time it changes the survey from the interview. The **Quick** depth does not ask this question.
