# Anonymous answers

An anonymous answer has no name and no email. AutoSurvey removes the contact questions. It links the answer to no record. A linked answer shows who answered, so you can follow up.

<Note>
Interview Mode is in development. It is not available in installed AutoSurvey orgs yet. This page tells you what the interview does when it is available.
</Note>

## Before you start

This topic belongs to the feature **Anonymous surveys**. An admin turns it on in AutoSurvey Setup. When the feature is off, the interview shows the topic as turned off. A choice for an anonymous survey then goes to the **Set up next** list as a feature card. The card says that the feature is turned off in this org, and its link is **Open AutoSurvey Setup**.

## When the interview asks

The interview asks this question when your brief is about staff, health or a confidential topic, or when it says that you know the people who answer.

The question is: **Link the answers to the person, or keep them anonymous?** The screen selects the recommended choice first. You can click a different choice.

## The choices

| Choice | What you see | What AutoSurvey changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Anonymous** (recommended) | AutoSurvey does not store who answered. Contact questions are removed. | Keeps the value "anonymous" on the draft. At Finish, the AI can propose change cards that remove questions that ask for a name or an email. |
| **Linked to the person** | AutoSurvey keeps who answered. You can follow up with the person. | No setting changes. |

This topic uses the AI at **Finish**. The AI gets your answer and can propose to remove or reword questions. Each proposal is a change card that you keep or skip.

## The Set up next card

The card **Share the results with care** shows when you chose **Anonymous** and your brief names staff, employees, team members, colleagues, workers or personnel.

| Card part | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **What and why** | Staff answer anonymously. Share only summary results, so nobody can find who gave an answer. |
| **Who** | You, or an admin |
| **Link** | **Open the results**. It opens the survey workspace with this survey. |

## Limits

- The choice **Anonymous** does not change a survey setting or a release setting. AutoSurvey can still link a response to a person, for example a response from an invitation. Only the questions change, and only for the change cards that you keep.
- Check the questions yourself before you send the survey.
