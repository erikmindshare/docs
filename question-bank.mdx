# Question bank

AutoSurvey finds questions in your bank that match this survey. The same wording keeps reports comparable over time.

<Note>
Interview Mode is in development. It is not available in installed AutoSurvey orgs yet. This page tells you what the interview does when it is available.
</Note>

## When the interview asks

The interview asks this question when your org has a question bank and some questions in it match this survey. The topic is in the group **Survey basics**. It does not show in the list of all topics.

The question is: **Reuse wordings from your question bank?** The screen selects the recommended choice first. You can click a different choice.

## The choices

| Choice | What you see | What AutoSurvey changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Yes, reuse my bank wordings** (recommended) | AutoSurvey uses bank wordings where they match. | No setting changes. The answer goes to the AI at **Finish**. |
| **No** | AutoSurvey writes new wordings. | No setting changes. The answer goes to the AI at **Finish**. |

This topic uses the AI at **Finish**. The AI gets your answer and its result text. It can reword, remove or add questions, change the answer options and change a question type. Each proposal is a change card that you keep or skip. If the AI proposes nothing, no change card shows.

## Limits

- The AI does not get the questions in your bank. It cannot copy a bank wording.
- To use a bank question, add it from the question library in the survey builder. See [Question library and versioning](/concepts/question-library-and-versioning).
