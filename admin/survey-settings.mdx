# Survey Settings

Open a survey and click the **Settings** tab. It shows five cards: **How it runs**, **Answers**, **Scoring & alerts**, **Data** and **The questions sheet**. Each card shows its current values on one line. Click anywhere on a card to open it, change its controls, then click **Save** (or **Cancel**). One card is open at a time. Each card has a **Learn more** link to its guide.

Everyone who can see the survey can read the settings. A card opens only for a person with deploy access. If you can read but not edit, the tab says so; ask an AutoSurvey admin to make the change.

## When a change reaches a respondent

| Card | When it applies |
| --- | --- |
| **How it runs** | At the next release. |
| **Answers** | At the next release. |
| **Scoring & alerts** | At the next release. |
| **Data › Record data in questions** | At the next release. |
| **Data › Answers written to records** | At the next release. |
| **Data › Reporting object** | At once, when you confirm the switch. |
| **Data › Description** | At once (authors only; respondents never see it). |
| **The questions sheet** | At once. |

The settings that apply "at the next release" are copied onto the release when you mint it, and the survey runner reads the release. A live survey therefore keeps running with the settings it was released with until you release again. On a survey that has never been released, the card says **Applies when you release**.

## How it runs

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Screen flow** | A screen is a page of the survey. **Adapt to device** (recommended): one question per screen on phones, grouped pages on desktop. **Grouped pages**: several questions per page on every device. **One per screen**: one question per screen on every device. |
| **Auto-advance** | After a rating, a scale or a single choice, the survey moves to the next question by itself. A text answer waits for **Next**. With **One per screen** this happens on every device; with **Adapt to device** it happens on phones only. It is not available with **Grouped pages**: the switch is off and cannot be changed. |
| **Long text answers: default lines** | How tall a long-text box is when the screen opens (1 to 30 lines). It grows as the person types. A question can set its own height in its Display properties; that setting wins for that question. |

## Answers

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Strict validation** | **On**: a long-text answer must fit its length limit, and a person who picks "Other" must type what they mean before they can continue. **Off**: those two checks do not run. Everything else — required questions, number ranges, formats — is always checked. |
| **Skip validation while testing** | While you test, you can skip questions and leave required ones empty. This applies to previews and test runs only. It never reaches a released survey: a respondent is always validated. |
| **Identify the respondent** | **Off**: no registration step. **Optional**: a registration step (name and email) shows after the person finishes, and they can skip it. **Required**: the person must register before the first question. Registered responses show the person on the submission. **Optional** and **Required** promise the person a results report, so the survey needs a delivery date first: a scheduled close date or a report cadence, both set on the Send tab. |

## Scoring & alerts

This card decides when a response is marked negative or positive, and shows exactly which follow-up Flows run on that mark and whether each Flow is active. The rules and the Flows are described on their own page: [Scoring and alerts](/admin/scoring-and-alerts).

## Data

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Reporting object** | Answers are always saved in AutoSurvey and show on the Results tab. The reporting object is an extra copy of each response for Salesforce reports. The card shows the object name and **On** (responses are also copied to it) or **Off** (responses are not copied). The object name is set when the survey is created and cannot change. The link **View the fields in Setup** opens that object in Setup. A survey with no reporting object shows **None**. |
| **Turn off… / Turn on…** | Turns the copy to the reporting object off or on for this survey. You confirm first. **Off**: new responses are not copied, a release does not create or update the object, and the "Reporting needs repair" warning stops. Nothing is deleted. **On**: the reporting setup creates or updates the object, its fields and its permission sets (now on a released survey, else at the next release), and new responses are copied. Responses saved while it was off are not copied. |
| **Record data in questions** | A question can show a value from the record the survey is about, with a tag such as `%%record.Account.Name%%`. Only the fields listed here can be shown; a field a respondent must not see stays off this list. Applies at the next release. |
| **Answers written to records** | When a survey is about a record (a Case, a Contact), an answer or a score can be written onto that record when the response is submitted. Click **Add mapping**, then pick the **Source** (**Answer** or **Score**), the **Question** or **Score**, the object it is **Written to**, and the **Field**. Only a field you can edit yourself can be chosen. Click **Save** in this section to keep the mappings. Applies at the next release. |
| **Description** | An authors' note. It is shown in the survey list and the sheet monitor. It is not shown to respondents. |

## The questions sheet

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Sheet** | The Google Sheet this survey's questions come from, with the time of the last sync. Click the sheet's name to open it. **Change sheet…** points the survey at another sheet. |
| **Who has access** | **See who has access** lists the people the sheet is shared with, and their role. |
| **Share with someone** | **Share the sheet…** shares the sheet with a person. Google may otherwise ask them for access when they open it; sharing here removes that step. Applies at once. |

A survey with no questions sheet says so; its questions are edited on the Questions tab. **Connect a sheet…** connects one. Sheet sync is an advanced option: the **Sheet sync** button in the survey header and the **Sheet sync** group in the survey's **⋯** menu hold the other sheet actions.
