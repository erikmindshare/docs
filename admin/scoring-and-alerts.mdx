# Scoring and Alerts

The **Scoring & alerts** card on a survey's **Settings** tab decides when a response is marked **negative** or **positive**, and shows the follow-up Flows that act on that mark. The rules apply at the next release; the marks are written when a response completes.

## How a response gets its score

A response is scored from its **NPS**, **CSAT** and **CES** questions. An author sets a question's type in its Display properties (**Metric type**) and marks one question as the **Primary metric**. Each type has its own scale and its own bands:

| Type | Default scale | Bands |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **NPS** | 0 to 10 | Promoter (9–10), Passive (7–8), Detractor (0–6) |
| **CSAT** | 1 to 5 | Satisfied (4–5), Neutral (3), Dissatisfied (1–2) |
| **CES** | 1 to 7 | Low effort (6–7), Medium effort (4–5), High effort (1–3). A higher CES score means less effort. |

A question can set its own scale maximum; the bands scale with it. A plain **Rating** question does not count toward the score: its meaning depends on the question, so a low rating is not treated as negative feedback. If you want a rating-style question to count, type it as CSAT.

Two values drive the rules:

- **The main score** — the band of the response's Primary metric question.
- **The average** — the mean of the NPS, CSAT and CES answers, as a percentage of their scales. A response with no NPS, CSAT or CES answer has no average.

## The two rules

Each rule has two signals. Tick one or both; if both are ticked, either signal fires the rule.

| Rule | Signal 1: the band | Signal 2: the average |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Negative response** | The main score lands in its lowest band: NPS Detractor, CSAT Dissatisfied or CES High effort. | The average falls **below** the threshold you enter (in percent). |
| **Positive response** | The main score lands in its top band: NPS Promoter, CSAT Satisfied or CES Low effort. | The average rises **above** the threshold you enter (in percent). |

Notes on the threshold:

- Leave the threshold empty and only the band counts. An empty threshold never fires.
- The negative signal is strictly below: a threshold of 40 does not fire at exactly 40.
- The positive signal is strictly above: a threshold of 85 does not fire at exactly 85.
- Choose **Do nothing** on a rule to turn it off: no mark and no follow-up.

## What the mark is

When a rule fires, the response record gets a mark:

| Field on the response | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Is negative response** | The negative rule fired. |
| **Is positive response** | The positive rule fired. |

The marks are ordinary fields on the response, so you can report and filter on them. The response also carries its primary score, its band and the average.

## Exactly what happens next

AutoSurvey ships three follow-up Flows. Each one starts when a response is created or updated with the matching mark.

| Flow | Starts when | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **AutoSurvey: Negative Feedback Recovery Case** | Is negative response is true, the survey is not about a Case, and the response has a contact. | Opens a Case for the contact and sends the contact's owner an in-app notification. |
| **AutoSurvey: Negative Case Feedback Follow-Up** | Is negative response is true. | For a survey sent about a Case (the survey's record is a Case, or the invitation is related to one): adds a Task on that Case and notifies the Case owner. It does nothing when no Case is found, and it does not add a second Task while an open follow-up Task exists. |
| **AutoSurvey: Promoter Review Request** | Is positive response is true and the response has a contact. | Creates a Task, emails the person a request for a public review, and notifies the contact's owner. |

### The Flows ship as templates

All three Flows are installed **inactive** (in Draft). Nothing happens on a mark until an admin activates them:

1. Open **Setup → Flows** (the Settings card has an **Open Flows in Setup** link).
2. Open the Flow, review it, and click **Activate**.

Before you activate **AutoSurvey: Promoter Review Request**, replace the placeholder review link in its email with your own review page. The template ships with a placeholder link on purpose.

The Settings card shows each Flow's status next to its name: **Active**, **Template · not active**, or **Status not readable here** when the person viewing the card cannot read Flow status in Setup.
