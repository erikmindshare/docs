Distribution settings govern **how a survey is delivered**: whether respondents are asked who they
are, what they see after submitting, when the results report is sent, and what AutoSurvey writes into
your CRM for people who aren't already there.

<Note>
**Where this lives.** Open a suite's workspace → **Send** → the **⚙️ Distribution settings**
drawer. Each control has an **ⓘ** help bubble whose **Learn more** link points back to the matching
section on this page.
</Note>

## At a glance

| Group | What it decides | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Identify respondent](#identify-respondent) | Whether respondents are asked who they are | **Optional** |
| [Respondent-facing results](#respondent-facing-results) | Whether respondents see an aggregate after submitting | **Off** |
| [Report contract](#report-contract) | The close date that unlocks email capture and the results report | **Required for email capture** |
| [Materialization policy](#materialization-policy) | What AutoSurvey creates in CRM for unmatched recipients | **Lead** (admin) / **None** (self-serve) |

<Tip>
**Scope is shown honestly.** Every group displays its **scope owner** — org, suite, per-form, or
per-send — and renders **"Mixed"** rather than a single fabricated value when the setting differs
across the forms or sends in scope. A per-send policy is never displayed as if it were one global
switch.
</Tip>

## Identify respondent

Controls whether a respondent is asked to identify themselves, and when.

| Option | What the respondent experiences | Anonymity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Off** | Never asked who they are. | Fully anonymous. |
| **Optional** *(default)* | Asked **after** they finish. Answers stay anonymous **unless** they choose *"identify my response."* | Anonymous by default; respondent opts in. |
| **Required** | Must identify **before** they can start. | Identified. |

**Linkage is always disclosed on screen.** When identification is optional, the default *results
subscription* stores the respondent's email **unlinked** from their answers — so someone can subscribe
to the results report without deanonymizing their response.

<Warning>
**Gated by close date.** *Optional* and *Required* both capture respondent email, so they stay
**disabled until a [close date](#report-contract) is declared** under Report contract. *Off* captures
no email and is always available.
</Warning>

## Respondent-facing results

Optionally shows respondents a snapshot of everyone's answers **after** they submit.

- **Show respondents an aggregate** — after submitting, the respondent sees a **frozen "largest group"
  teaser**: a simple summary of how everyone answered. It's a snapshot, not a live-updating dashboard.
- **Hero question** — picks **which** question that teaser summarizes. It's **overridable before the
  first publish only**, then **locked**, so the teaser can't silently change meaning after respondents
  have already seen it.

<Note>
**Default off.** The master switch defaults **off**, and **sensitive templates lock it off** so
aggregate results can never be exposed for a survey that shouldn't share them.
</Note>

## Report contract

The **close date** is the linchpin that unlocks email capture and schedules the results report.

- A **declared close date** lets AutoSurvey **capture respondent email** and **send the results report
  at close**.
- **No respondent email is captured** without a declared close date or cadence — this is why the
  email-capturing [Identify](#identify-respondent) modes gate on it.
- **Cancelling** a close date means AutoSurvey either **auto-sends** the report or
  **notifies-and-purges** captured contact data, so email never lingers without a contract to justify
  it.

<Tip>
**Default: required for email capture.** You can run a fully anonymous, no-email survey with no close
date. The moment you want to email respondents or send a results report, a close date becomes
mandatory.
</Tip>

## Materialization policy

Decides **what AutoSurvey creates in your CRM** for each recipient who isn't already there. This is a
**per-send** policy with an org default — not a single global switch.

| Option | For an unmatched recipient, AutoSurvey creates… |
| --- | --- |
| **Lead** *(admin-send default)* | a **Lead**. |
| **Contact** | a **Contact**. |
| **None** *(self-registration default)* | **nothing** — the response is recorded without a CRM record. |

**Create Leads from self-registrations** is a separate toggle, **off by default**, with its own consent
copy and volume caps — because self-service sign-ups are a different trust boundary than a list you
sent to.

<Warning>
**Never silent.** Admin-send Lead creation is always previewed: the **Add & Send** step shows
**"will create N Leads"** before anything is written, so the count is never a surprise.
</Warning>
