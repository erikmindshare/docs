# Fill in what you know

AutoSurvey takes values that you have, such as a region or an account, from the contact record. The survey does not ask these questions.

<Note>
Interview Mode is in development. It is not available in installed AutoSurvey orgs yet. This page tells you what the interview does when it is available.
</Note>

<Warning>
This feature is not available yet. The interview does not ask this question in any org today. The text above tells you what the topic is for. This page shows what the interview has ready for when the feature is released.
</Warning>

## Status

- AutoSurvey does not register a **prefilled-fields** feature in this release. The interview treats a topic with an unknown feature as not installed, and it never asks a topic that is not installed. The topic also does not show in the list of all topics.
- This topic changes no survey setting. No choice makes a change card.
- Prefilled fields are not in AutoSurvey yet.

## The question and the choices

When the feature is released, the interview asks: **Fill in what you already know, and not ask it?** The interview is to ask it when your brief says that you know the people, for example "we already know their region".

| Choice | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| **Yes, fill in what I know** (recommended) | AutoSurvey removes the questions and fills them from the record. |
| **No** | AutoSurvey asks every question. |
