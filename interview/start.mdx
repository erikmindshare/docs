---
title: Start an interview
description: Start Interview Mode from a brief in your own words or from a list of questions that you paste. Select how many questions the AI asks you with the three depths.
---

You know what you want to learn, but you do not have the questions. Or you have the questions, and you do not know the best settings. In the two cases, you start in the same box. The AI writes the survey first. Then it asks you about the survey.

## Start from your words

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the box">
    Click **New Survey**. The screen asks **What do you want to learn?**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the brief">
    Write it as you say it to a colleague. A good brief names the moment, what you want to learn, and what you do with the answers. Click **See good briefs** to see three examples. Each example has a **Use** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the depth">
    Below the box is the line **After the draft, the AI asks you about the survey:** with three buttons: **Quick**, **Standard** and **Thorough**. See [The three depths](#the-three-depths).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Write my survey">
    The AI writes the survey. Then the interview opens, and the first question shows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Example of a brief:

> When a support case closes, ask the customer how it went. I want to learn if the problem is solved, how fast we answered, and what we should do better. Keep it under two minutes.

A brief can have a maximum of 20,000 characters.

## Start from your questions

Paste your list of questions into the same box. When the text looks like a list of questions, this note shows: "This looks like a list of questions. The AI keeps your wording and picks the question types."

- To keep your wording, click **Write my survey**.
- If the text is a brief and not a list, click **Use it as a brief**.

The interview then opens as it does for a brief.

## The three depths

The depth sets how many questions the interview asks you. It does not change the AI model, so it does not change the quality of one answer or the cost of one AI call.

| Depth | Questions about the goal of the survey | What else changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Quick** | A maximum of 3. The AI asks only for facts that it needs and that only you know. | The fewest questions. The interview also asks about a maximum of 3 features, and the look. It does not ask about words to avoid. |
| **Standard** | A maximum of 5. | No other change. |
| **Thorough** | A maximum of 8. | The most questions, for a more detailed survey. |

**Standard** is selected when you open the box.

You can ask for more questions at the end of each depth. On the last screen of the interview, click **Ask me more**. Each click is one AI call, and the AI asks a maximum of 3 more questions. The interview stores a maximum of 14 questions about the goal for one survey.

### Change the depth later

The start card on the **Review** screen has the list **Interview depth**. The list has **Quick: only the facts the survey needs** and **Standard**. If the survey uses **Thorough**, the list shows that depth too. A change applies to the next questions that the AI selects.

## If you do not want the questions now

Click **Skip this** next to the depth buttons. The AI writes the survey, and the interview does not open. This choice applies to this survey only.

To put the step back before you click **Write my survey**, click **Put this step back** or **Ask me the questions**.

You can also start the interview later. The **Review** screen has the card **Would you like help refining this survey?** with these buttons:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Answer a few questions** (Recommended) | Starts the interview. After you used the interview one time, the button is **Resume interview**. |
| **Overall instructions** | Opens a text box. You say what to change in your own words, and the AI changes the survey. See [Answer, update and undo](/interview/answer-update-undo#overall-instructions). |
| **Start over** | Erases all the questions and makes one AI call to build a new survey. |

## What the AI can offer before the first question

For a complex survey, the AI can show one or two offers before the first question.

**More questions.** The panel says: "This survey looks complex. Do you want the AI to ask more questions?" It lists the reasons.

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Yes, ask more** | The AI asks up to 3 more questions now. This is one AI call. |
| **No, keep it as it is** | The interview continues with the questions that it has. |

**The Premium AI level for this survey.** If the org has an active AI level with the name Premium, the panel asks: "Build it on the Premium tier?" It lists the reasons. When AutoSurvey has a cost estimate, the panel shows the cost of each AI run.

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Yes, use Premium** | A stronger AI model makes the interview plan, the changes and any rebuild of this survey. Only this survey uses it. |
| **No, keep my tier** | The interview continues with the AI model of your current AI level. |
