---
title: Refine a survey that exists
description: Use Interview Mode on a survey that is created, and learn what the interview can change after the first release of a survey.
---

A survey is created, and then the plan changes: the team wants one more question, a different tone, or a reminder. You do not want to build the survey again. The interview can open on a survey that exists. It reads the survey as it is now, and each update changes the survey.

## Open the interview on a survey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the survey">
    Go to the **Questions** tab of the survey.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the panel">
    The panel **Refine this survey** is above the list of questions. It says: "Answer a few questions, and AutoSurvey updates the survey. You can undo each change."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select how to refine">
    Click **Answer a few questions (recommended)** to start the interview. Or click **Overall instructions** to say what to change in your own words.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After you answered questions one time, the panel has **Review my answers** and **Answer more questions**. After an update, its title is **Refine again**.

The interview works as it does for a new survey. See [Answer, update and undo](/interview/answer-update-undo). Three things are different:

- The update writes to the survey, not to a draft. The interview stays open after the update, so you see the change list, **Not applied** and **Undo**.
- **Return to survey** closes the interview.
- If a different person or a different screen changed the questions after the interview read them, the update is refused. Open the interview again. This protects the work of the other person.

The panel shows only when you can edit the questions of the survey.

## Before the first release

Before the first release, the interview can add, change and remove questions. One exception: AutoSurvey does not remove a question that has answers.

A survey that has a Google Sheet is a special case. Before the first release, the panel says: "The questions of this survey are in its Google Sheet. Until the first release, AutoSurvey writes each change to the Sheet."

## After the first release

A released survey has answers, reports and fields that use its questions. Thus the interview can make only these changes:

| The interview can | The interview cannot |
| --- | --- |
| Change a setting, for example reminders, the close date or the bot check. | Add a question. |
| Change the text of a question. | Remove a question. |
| | Change the type of a question. |
| | Change the choices of a question. Stored answers, scores and show and hide rules use the values of the choices. |

The interview shows only the features that are settings. A feature that only changes the wording of the survey is not in the list.

If an update or an Undo needs a bigger change, AutoSurvey refuses it with this message: "This survey is released. The interview can change settings and the text of questions. Make a new survey for a bigger change." No part of the survey changes.

For a bigger change, the message tells you to make a new survey.

## Surveys that the interview does not open

| Survey | Message |
| --- | --- |
| A released survey that has a Google Sheet | "This survey has a Google Sheet. Change its questions in the Sheet." |
| A survey that has audiences or rounds | The panel shows a message that the interview cannot change the questions of this survey. The message starts with "This survey has audiences or rounds." |
| A survey with no questions that the interview can read | "This survey has no questions that the interview can read." |
