A survey with the standard colors does not look like your company. But you do not want to set each color and each font before you know the questions. In the interview, one step gives you a small number of complete looks for this survey. You select one, and you can change each detail later.

## The step Select a design

The step shows a list of looks. A line above the list says how many looks there are and which one the AI recommends for this survey.

Each look shows a small sample with its colors, and the fonts for **Headings** and for **Questions and answers**.

| On a look | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Recommended** | The AI selected this look for your survey. |
| **Customized** | The look has changes that the AI made from your description. |
| **Standard look** | The default. No design is saved with the survey. It is the last look in the list. |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click a look">
    The recommended look is selected when the step opens. Click a different look to select it. A look can also have a list **Heading font**. If the list does not show the standard look, click **More looks**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Keep this design">
    The interview goes to the next step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can change every detail of the design later, on the **Design** page of the survey.

## Describe your own look

If no look is correct, click **None of these? Describe your own look with AI**.

1. In the box **Describe the look you want**, type a description. Example: warmer colours and a serif heading.
2. Click **Customize with AI**.

The AI selects one of the looks and changes some of its values. The screen shows the result, and the line **AI reason:** says why. If AutoSurvey could not use a value from the AI, the list **AutoSurvey did not use these values:** shows the value and the reason. The look is not used until you click **Keep this design**.

## A header picture and an icon

The same step can ask: **Add an AI header picture and icon after Create?** The question shows only when AI pictures are available to you.

| Choice | What occurs |
| --- | --- |
| **Yes** | After Create, AutoSurvey makes a header picture and an icon. This uses 2 credits. The pictures appear in **Design** in about 1 to 3 minutes. You can change them on the **Design** page. |
| **No** | No pictures. You can make pictures later on the **Design** page. |

For some surveys, the step also asks: **What look do you want? Name a style, colours or a subject.** The answer can have a maximum of 255 characters.

## What the AI does, and what you decide

- The AI recommends a look and can change a look from your description.
- You select the look. The recommended look is a suggestion: select **Standard look** if you want no design.
- AutoSurvey makes pictures only when you select **Yes**.
